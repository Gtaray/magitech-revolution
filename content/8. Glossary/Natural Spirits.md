The land is filled with nature spirits of all kinds that inhabit the forests, rivers, deserts, mountains, and skies. There are so many spirits that no mystic could name them all, though no one would really want to. There are a few spirits that have reached some level of notoriety, enough that even non-mystical folks might call upon or curse their names. These spirits are:

## Relentless Gaze of the Sun
Often maligned, this is perhaps the most well-known spirit. It is the spirit of the sun that scorches the land. It's heat is immense, and it is, at best, uncaring. At worst, it wishes to scour the world clean, burning all under its relentless gaze.

Perhaps in defiance of such hostility, [[8. Glossary/Relic Engine\|Relic Engines]] are used to bind and generate energy from this spirit's ceaseless inferno.

## Downpour Drenches the World
A spirit of rain. Downpour Drenches the World is a divisive spirit; some see it as the balm that heals the land from Relentless Gaze of the Sun's rays, while others see it as a destructive force that seeks to wash away all trace of civilization. It is likely both, at least partly.

Downpour Drenches the World is the spirit of rainstorms and flash floods, both common in the summer when Relentless Gaze of the Sun is at its most destructive.

## Lightning Splits the Sky
Some mystics consider Lightning Splits the Sky to be the youngest of the six major spirits, though no one else really knows what what means or why it might be important. It is a mercurial spirit that only shows itself occasionally, though when it does it is always an event: violent lightning storms that blast the earth and start wildfires.

Smaller lightning storms are this spirits favorite way to play; sometimes it will send lightning streaking across the sky for no other reason than to enjoy the colors it makes.

Lightning Split the Sky is another common spirit whose power is bound and controlled for the purpose of magitech. [[8. Glossary/Spark\|Sparks]] are the main result of such alchemy, though bottled lightning is used to a lesser extent in almost all bits of magitech.

## Unforgetting Forests
The forests of Duradin are a wild and mystical place. While they are not considered wodes proper (those are cursed fae things), they can be equally inscrutable. This is attributed to any number of spirits, but the one most thought of is Unforgetting Forests. "The forest remembers" is a common warning against acts against nature.

It's not all danger, though. Many rituals and rites rely on Unforgetting Forests' eternal memory. Someone looking for help might venture into the forest and call upon it for advice. Funeral services are often in the forests, since that's where the memories of the departed are strongest. And beseeching the trees for lost knowledge is a common enough trope that folks know that it all knowledge comes with a hidden price or twist.

## Stone's Unyielding Endurance
Stone's Unyielding Endurance is the most ancient of all the spirits; many consider it to predate the planet. In some myths [[8. Glossary/Gods#Ord\|Ord]] communed with this spirit and drew on its power to first sculpt the earth. Stone's Unyielding Endurance is not a spirit a mortal might commune with. It does not answer to any calls, but then again it doesn't seem to have any interest in the dealings of the mortals that live upon its surface. Very rarely is its presence felt, but it is always there.

This spirit's energies are used when forging infused metals, namely [[8. Glossary/Adamantine]] and [[8. Glossary/Duranium]]. It's essence is also bound and used to power [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuits]].
