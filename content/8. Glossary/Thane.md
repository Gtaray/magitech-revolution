The most powerful singular individual in all of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] is the thane. The thane, who is a monarch based on lineage, is an almost entirely separate branch of the government to the [[8. Glossary/Grand Council]]. 

The title is passed down from one thane to the next, usually from parent to child, but it has not been uncommon for thanes to pass the title to a sibling, niece, or nephew. As a measure to avoid wars of succession, thanes have a term limit of 50 years, and both the [[8. Glossary/Grand Council]] and church anoint a new thane.

The current thane is Hruun [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Ironroot Clan\|Ironroot]] IV, who is 38 years into his term.
