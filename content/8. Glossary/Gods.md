# Ord
The eldest sibling of the three Duradin gods. Ord, The Father, is the god of wisdom, knowledge, law, justice, society, civilization, etc.

In most myths, Ord brought order to chaos in order to sculpt and craft the planet on which everyone lives.

# Torm
The Duradin god of strength, battle, and victory. The middle sibling of Ord and Helm.

# Helm
The youngest sibling of the three Duradin gods. Helm is the god of creation, imagination, and ingenuity.
