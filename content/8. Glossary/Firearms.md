No bit of magitech is as widely adopted as the gun. Firearms were first invented two centuries ago, only then they used black powder as the mechanism with which to propel a metal slug at lethal velocities. This idea, however, was quickly abandoned; in a world where magical fire is a commonly conjured weapon, carrying around a sachet of explosive and volatile powder was a terrible idea. Firearms were an interesting footnote in history, but nothing more.

That is until 40 years ago when some enterprising alchemists reinvented what a firearm was. Black powder was out, and in its place firearms were powered by careful constructed catalyst spells. The ammunition itself would be imbued and etched with magic that the gun activates. The simplest and most common spell used is the humble magic missile, which was modified to carry the ammunition. This solved all safety concerns and made firearms something truly anyone could use.

The trade off is that making guns is a lot more involved, and requires expertise that its inventors are not keen on sharing. Ammunition is costly, and repairing a damaged gun is more involved. But that didn't matter. After it's invention the tech spread like wildfire throughout [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Even people who don't like magitech consider carrying a firearm. Entire industries have sprung up around modifying guns and making custom ammunition. Outside of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] the tech is still somewhat of a mystery, though that is certain to change sooner rather than later.
