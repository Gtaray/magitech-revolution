The invention of the relic engine is what sparked the current magitech revolution. It is a self-contained vessel about the size of a large footlocker that outputs reliable, safe, and clean magical energy that can power any number of different magitech devices. It is a marvel of modern science, combining cutting edge materials engineering and alchemy to create a vessel that can bind, contain, and extract natural energy from bound elementals. A relic engine discharges its energy over time as it is used, and then requires downtime to recharge its stores.

Despite the wonder of it, there are concerns with relic engines. Their power relies on bound elemental spirits which many druids take issue with and see it as tantamount to slavery.

Smaller airships (patrol boats, light fighters, etc) might only have a small single core engine such that the ships need to land and shut down to recharge.
