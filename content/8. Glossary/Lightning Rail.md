A multi-car, high-speed train that runs north and south, mostly through vast stretches of desert. It is the fastest and most reliable way to transport a lot of cargo between [[Durvos]], [[Torhmek]], and [[Ladur]]. It is powered by a bound lightning elemental.

Despite its name, the train does not actually run on a rail. Instead it levitates along a line of magnetic pylons.
