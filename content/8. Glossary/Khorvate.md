[[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]] is composed of four regions, called Khorvates, each of which is ruled by a particular ancient, and very large, [[Clan|clan]]. Each Khorvate has a singular ruler called a [[Khorvar]], which is sometimes the [[Magnar]] of the ruling [[Clan|clan]], but not always. Sometimes the ruling [[Clan|clan]] will hold elections to determine the [[Khorvar]], and sometimes the [[Magnar]] appoints one.

Khorvates have a seat of power, usually its largest city, from which the khorvate is governed by a council of varying size.

Because a Khorvate is ruled by a singular, very large, very powerful [[Clan|clan]], all other clans that live within the Khorvate implicitly accept to be governed by the ruling Khorvate, but they are under no obligation to serve them in any capacity. [[Clan|Clans]] generally do not owe fealty to other clans, though marriage pacts, trade deals, and alliances are quite common.

Very often disputes will occur when a Khorvate enacts a decree that smaller clans disagree with. Those smaller clans are under no compulsion to follow the decree. It's up to the [[Khorvar]] to convince smaller clans to go along with their plans. Very often this is resolved with a simple trade deal. Rarely it ends in bloodshed.

There are currently four khorvates:

- [[Ladur]]
- [[Durvos]]
- [[Torhmek]]
- [[Oghrann]]
