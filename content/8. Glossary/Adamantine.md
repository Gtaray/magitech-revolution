A dull gunmetal grey metal found deep in the earth. It's melting point is so high that only molten lava can be used to heat and work the metal. When refined, the metal has a supernatural ability to absorb kinetic energy, making it especially useful for weapons and armor by reducing the impact shock a wearer or wielder would feel.

Because of its hardiness, adamantine does not take well to etching and engraving, making enchanted adamantine quite rare. It is also nearly impossible to alloy with other metals, with the only known alloy being [[8. Glossary/Duranium]], made by alloying it with [[8. Glossary/Mithril]].
