You've got an axe to grind against the powers that be. Could be you sympathize with the locals getting squeezed by the major players. Maybe a constable put you in prison for a crime you didn't commit. Or maybe you just don't like how much bleeding there is on the edge of their tech.

Whatever your reason, you fight against the status quo. You are rebels, renegades, and in the eyes of civilized society, terrorists.

**When you play this crew, you earn xp when you complete a job that sabotages, subverts, or otherwise impairs the operations of the government.**

*To what end do you fight? Where do you call 'home', and who are your allies there? Who are the 'real' criminals?*

# Special Abilities
### Old Hands
When you’re at [[6. Factions/3. Faction Status#Faction Status Levels\|War (-3)]] with any major political faction, all crew members get **-1d** to **vice** rolls and still get two **[[5. Downtime/4. Downtime Activities\|downtime activities]]** instead of just one.

*Rolling one fewer die means you're less likely to overindulge.*

### Forged in Fire
Your crew has been toughened by cruel experience. When you **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/2. Push Yourself]]** to avoid or reduce consequences, you each get **+1d**.

*This ability applies to all the PCs in the crew.*

### Sympathizers
Your ideology is especially appealing. When you deal with a crew or [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]], the GM will tell you who among them believes in your cause (one, a few, many, or all).

*If the GM is unsure of the answer, they can make a fortune roll using the crew’s quality, perhaps modified by their standing with a faction. Factions are not monolithic and while the faction as a whole (and certain individuals within it) may despise the PCs, some of their members may still have rebellious leanings. Note that the answer to the question of believers is always at least one.*

### Natural Enemies
When you run a job against government factions, take **+1d** to the **[[4. The Job/2. Planning & Engagement#Engagement Roll\|engagement roll]]** roll.

*This applies to any job where your primary target is one of the major government factions. If the job inconveniences a faction but doesn’t involve their members, the bonus does not apply.*

### Spark of Rebellion
If you leave a calling card or a highly visible symbol of resistance on your job, gain **+2 [[4. The Job/6. Heat]]**. Ignore up to **2 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** you would spend during the next **[[5. Downtime/1. Downtime]]**, as your sympathizers cover some of your expenses.

*When you take this ability, discuss what the symbol of your rebellion is. When you leave behind your calling card, it should be prominent or sure to be found.*

### Just Cause
When your crew does the right thing at cost to themselves, you may mark a **[[3. The Crew/6. Crew Advancement\|crew xp]]**.

*The cost to your crew must be real, though it need not be devastating. Losing an meaningful opportunity, experiencing a setback with a project, or angering a powerful faction could all count.*

### Hearts & Minds
Each crew member may add 1 action rating to **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Command]]**, **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Consort]]**, or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Sway]]** (up to a max of 3).

*Each player may choose the action they prefer (you don’t all have to choose the same one). If you take this ability during initial character and crew creation, it supersedes the normal starting limit for action ratings.*

# Crew Upgrades
### Black Market Contacts
Able to get you all the parts and modules (even illegal ones) your ship needs, even when you’re **[[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted]]**. Illegal modules (as usual) may require you to run a job to acquire them. Your contact may know where to find them, and give you a leg up, but it’s up to you to get things that just aren’t available at any price (prototypes, rare devices, and incredibly illegal things).

### Blessed
Some people are just plain lucky. The common folk think this is some sort of sign. Don’t look too much into it. You start with **+1 [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/5. Gambits\|gambit]]** at the start of every job. It costs two advancements to unlock this instead of the usual one.

### Drive
Each PC gets **+1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Trauma\|trauma]]** box. This can bring a PC with 4 trauma back into play if you wish. It costs three advancements to unlock this instead of the usual one.

### Popular Support
It takes work to win hearts and minds, but your cause has supporters among the common folk. When you approach a region or city, ask the GM who there might be a sympathizer. This may make escapes — and simply walking around while **[[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted]]** — much easier. It costs three advancements to unlock this instead of the usual one.

### Secret Base
Could be an ancient ruin. Maybe buildings inside a massive cave system. Possibly an old ghost-town on the frontier, long abandoned but now repurposed. You have found and commissioned a hiding spot away from watchful eyes where you and your allies can meet, hide, lick your wounds, and plan your jobs, all without the government finding out. It’s secret… for now.

# Contacts
- **Garn**, a Guild weapons engineer. Does his conscience not let him do all the Guild demands? Did he help you acquire your ship?
- **Ibawan**, a Druid. Perhaps a guide into dangerous ruins where you could hide?
- **Ida Black**, a famous performer. Darling of Duradin. Perhaps her tours can be good covers to sneak into secure areas?
- **Tiko**, a hotshot pilot. The best, but kicked out of the 21st Squadron. A getaway pilot for you?
- **Tyura**, a legendary assassin. How did you earn her loyalty? What is her beef with the guilds and clans?
