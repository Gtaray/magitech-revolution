You're a bunch of misfits, lost boys, outcasts, strangers, and weirdos. Your interests aren't necessarily in money, status, or ideals; your goals are far more esoteric, inscrutable, and downright bizarre.

When you are creating a Weirdos crew, spend some extra time defining what mysterious goal has brought you together. What do you intend to achieve collectively that you could not do on your own?

**When you play this crew, you earn xp when you complete a job that furthers your crew's unique goal.**

_How did you all meet? How does your flavor of weird conflict with your crewmates? Are there any ulterior motives?_

# Special Abilities
### The Means of Your Defeat
You get **+1d** to the **[[4. The Job/2. Planning & Engagement#Engagement Roll\|engagement roll]]** for **[[4. The Job/2. Planning & Engagement#Arcane\|arcane]]** plans.

_You have a handle on weird magic that no one expects, and can use that to get an advantage when on a job._

### Even We Know Not Our Plans
Your **[[4. The Job/3. Flashbacks\|flashbacks]]** have **potency** when they involve the bizarre and supernatural.

_This can mean that your flashbacks stretch the normal limit of what is "reasonable", or that their outcomes are particularly effective. How do you get this extra foresight?_

### We Do What We Must
Before anyone in your crew **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice\|induldges their vice]]**, they clear **1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress\|stress]]**, reducing the likelihood of overindulging.

_When your life is filled with bizarre magic and occult powers, small things like alcoholism or drug addiction aren't that bad (you hope)._

### The River Flows Ever Away
You know secret and occult rituals to hide the worst of your crimes, even from diviners and necromancers. You don't take extra **[[4. The Job/6. Heat\|Heat]]** when killing is involved in a job.

_Your rituals prevent spells like Locate Creature/Object, Speak with Dead, and Raise Dead. What do those rituals look like?_

### Bound in Darkness
You may use **[[4. The Job/4. Teamwork\|teamwork]] maneuvers** with any crew member, regardless of the distance separating you.

_By what occult means does your teamwork manifest over distance? How is it strange and disturbing? By what ritualistic method are members initiated into this ability?_

### Ours is the Forgotten Magic
Each PC may add 1 action rating to **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Attune]]**, **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor]]**, or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Hack]]** (up to a max of 3).

_Each player may choose the action they prefer (you don’t all have to choose the same one). If you take this ability during initial character and crew creation, it supersedes the normal starting limit for action ratings._

### A Vessel For My God
The supernatural beings your crew communes with sometimes manifest in the physical world. This can be a great boon, but the priorities and values of such beings are not those of mortals. 

_You have been warned._

# Crew Upgrades
### Ritual Sanctum
Your crew has access to a fixed ritual circle to perform secret magics. Gain **+1 tick** when **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work\|Working]]** on a project that involves weird and strange magic.

### Cursed Idol
A strange idol your crew carries that brings you luck. What does it ask for in return for its favors? You start with **+1 [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/5. Gambits\|gambit]]** at the start of every job. It costs two advancements to unlock this instead of the usual one.

### Offering Altar
Once per downtime, the crew may make an offering of significant value to some patron or elder power. If the offering is of sufficient value, mark a **[[3. The Crew/6. Crew Advancement\|crew xp]]**

### Bag of Holding
Everyone in your crew has a magical bag that shares an internal extra-dimensional space with all other bags. This bag is **0 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Loadout\|Load]]**, and can hold **2 Load** worth of gear. Anyone who has loaded their own Bag of Holding can access the contents of the bag. Like all bags of this type, turning it inside out empties its contents.

### Damaged
Each PC gets **+1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Trauma\|trauma]]** box. This can bring a PC with 4 trauma back into play if you wish. It costs three advancements to unlock this instead of the usual one.

# Contacts
- **Gagan**, an academic. What do they study? Are their interests aligned or opposed to yours?
- **Adki**, an occultist. A practitioner of lost magics. How do they fit in with your crew goal?
- **Moriya**, a spirit trafficker. Do they supply you, or you supply them? Have you double-crossed each other?
- **Hux**, an antiquarian. Sells _curiosities_ at bargain rates. Have they helped you or cursed you.
- **Ox**, an astronomer. Studies the heavens. Do you go to them to get answers or do they seek your downfall?
