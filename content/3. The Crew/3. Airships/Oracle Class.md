A decommissioned military support ship designed for reconnaissance. Though she's a smaller ship, she's got infrastructure ready to receive military modules.

She has the most potential **Support** of the starting airships, which means she's capable of carrying a wide array of different utilities, allowing for flexibility in how you gather information and approach your jobs. She also has the lowest possible **Hull**, so be careful when engaging other airships directly, as your staying power is limited.

This airship starts with an **Advanced Scanning Array**, **Bolt Tower**, and **High Efficiency Engines** installed, as well as the **Intruder Alarm** upgrade.
