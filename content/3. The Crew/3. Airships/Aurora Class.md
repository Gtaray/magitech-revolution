The Aurora is an experimental and as-of-yet unreleased chassis whose designs leaked. As such, nothing about this ship is standard; most normal repair shops have never seen the tech on this ship, so you're on your own for repairs and upgrades. That said, she's a dream to fly. Nimble and responsive, plus that unreleased and illegally bootlegged Blink Drive is pretty slick.

She's a small airship, and as such the maximum ratings of her systems are lower than other starting ships. She's perfect for a small crew, while larger crews might struggle to find things to do while in the air.

This airship starts with a **Blink Drive** and **Smuggling Compartments** installed, as well as the **Escape Hatch** upgrade.
