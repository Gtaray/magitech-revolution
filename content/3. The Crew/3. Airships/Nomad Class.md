A tinkerer's dream; hardly more than an open frame built around a cargo hold and engines. She's lightweight and nimble, especially if you upgrade the sails and fans, and is easy to repair in a pinch.

She has the most potential **Engine** of the starting airships, which means she can be the fastest ship out there, able to race with the best. She also has the lowest possible **Weapon**, so strategy and tactics must be employed to take on tougher opposition.

This airship starts with a **Cargo Hold**, **Afterburners**, and **Environment Stabilizer** installed, as well as the **Drop Hatch** upgrade.

# Ship Qualities
- **Crew Quality**: 0/3
- **Hull**: 1/3
- **Engines**: 1/4
- **Weapons**: 0/2
- **Support**: 1/3
