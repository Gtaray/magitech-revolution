Self explanatory. Note that most non-military ships in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] are limited to bolt throwers. Obvious weapons can land you in trouble, though powered down and hidden armaments may be regularly overlooked.

### Bolt Thrower
Standard ballista setup that looses heavy iron bolts. Effective and efficient.

### Grappling Hooks
Officially for loading cargo, it's an array of nets and grappling lines that can tie together two ship's for towing (or boarding).

### Javelin Field Cannon
A long-range, single shot heavy cannon whose large caliber ammunition can punch through even heavy armor. Treats a target's **Hull** as 1 rating less.

### Helm's Eye
Uses a series of lenses to focus light and incinerate targets. Not legal.

### Manticore
Looses a spray of small flachettes meant to damage a ship's delicate balloons and propellers. Does minimal structural damage to a ship, and has middling effectiveness against personnel. Not illegal, but also not something civilians should have.

### Mining Lance
A vicious close-range, high-powered energy drill meant to bore through rock and extract precious minerals. Easily modified to bore through hulls.

### Barking Dog Cannonade
A medium-range detonator launcher. Inaccurate compared to other weapons, and highly illegal due to how effectively it can deliver widespread devastation. Includes a handful of small-charge ordinance, but specialized detonators will have to be **loaded** separately. Illegal.

### Ord's Hammer
A capital-sized, fixed mounted cannon that can fire once before being reloaded (and probably repaired). May fry engines. Super illegal. Requires **Weapons** [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems#System Rating\|rating]] of 3+ and takes up 2 module slots

### Thaumogravic Pulse Cannon
Experimental (and illegal) weapon that magically dampens a target out to medium range, halting [[8. Glossary/Relic Engine\|relic engine]] and suppressing most low-level magitech. Does no physical damage, and does not hinder any mechanical systems. It is advised to avoid use at close-range.
