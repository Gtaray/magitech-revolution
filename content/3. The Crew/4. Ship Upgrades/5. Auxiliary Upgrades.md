Generalized upgrades that aren't strictly limited to one of the core four ship systems.

### Armory
A secure room holding the crew's weapons and armor. All crew weapons and armor are considered to be **masterwork** if not already so.

### Brig
Jail. Not meant for long-term incarceration. Will prevent most attempts to escape.

### Drop Hatch
Similar to an escape hatch in that it is a set of reinforced hatches on the underside of the hull that allows for safe exits from the ship regardless of altitude. The drop hatch's fall rate is not artificially limited (it's only limit is terminal velocity). Illegal without government licensure

### Escape Hatch
Reinforced hatches on the underside of the hull that allows those who depart through the hatch to fall safely to the ground at a rate of 10 ft/s

### Galley
A combined kitchen and serving area for meals. Greatly facilitates longer trips and passenger transport.

Everyone gets "Home cooking" as a **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice\|vice]]**. During the Downtime Phase the crew may collectively spend **1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** to allow any of the crew to indulge this vice together by enjoying a home-cooked, shared meal. If anyone **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice#Overindulgence\|overindulges]]**, roll on the chart below to see what complication the crew suffers:

1 - Brawl: Half the crew (rounded down) suffer a level 2 **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm]]**
2 - Wild: Damage a random [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems\|ship system]]
3 - Bad Idea: A randomly determined crew upgrade or equipment is unavailable for the next job.
4 - Indulgent: Spend **+1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** on a lavish feast
5 - Harmless Pranks: The crew all choose an item in their [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Loadout\|loadout]] that is not available for the next job.
6 - Harsh Words: The crew all suffer **1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]**

### Intruder Alarm
A full suite of protective spells meant to detect and rebuff intruders, tampering, or other undesired activities.

### Medical Bay
A relatively clean room with medical equipment. Not a real hospital, but sufficient to patch most injuries. Storage for drugs and medical tools. The crew's **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Recover]]** activities fill **+1 tick**.

### Power Reserves
A secondary relic engine that's got just enough juice to run the ship independently of the primary relic engine. Sufficient for a few days of operation at minimal power usage or an hour or two at full power. Also grants your ship an extra use of **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]**. Can be taken up to three times.

### Vault
Useful for securing valuables. Magical locks allows for personalized access. Uses **Hull** rating when contested.

### Workshop
A stock of metal and magitech components, micro-forge, and tools needed to build, modify, or disassemble complex devices, weapons, and tools. Adds **+1 quality** to **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Craft]]** rolls, and **+1 tick** for relevant **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work#Long-Term Project\|long-term projects]]**.
