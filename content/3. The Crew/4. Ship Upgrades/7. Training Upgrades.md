Training unlocks the crew's ability to mark their third action and fourth [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Actions\|Action Ratings]] for actions of a specific [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes\|attribute]]. Each training initially costs 2 advancements to unlock the third action rating, and an additional 2 to unlock the fourth action rating.

### Insight Training
- Once you have marked 2 upgrades: You have a veteran **instructor** (Quality rating 3) for [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Insight\|Insight]] actions. Your crew can each have one [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Insight\|Insight]] action whose rating is three. Must be [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]] 1 or higher to take.
- Once you have marked 4 upgrades: You have a master **instructor** (Quality rating 4) for [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Insight\|Insight]] actions. Your crew can each have two [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Insight\|Insight]] actions whose rating is three OR one action with a rating of 3 and one with a rating of 4. Must be [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]] 2 or higher to take.
- When a crew member gains a third or fourth action rating in an [[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Insight\|Insight]] action, mark a **[[3. The Crew/6. Crew Advancement\|crew xp]]**.
