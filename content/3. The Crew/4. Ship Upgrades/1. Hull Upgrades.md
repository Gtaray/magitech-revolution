Hull upgrades govern how tough an airship is. Hull modules are passive systems built throughout the ship and often are necessary to even allow certain actions.

### Cargo Hold

Enough space to carry a moderate (coin-earning) shipment. A cargo hold is evident when the ship is boarded, and no special precautions are taken to hide its contents.

### Crew Quarters

You can sleep anywhere, but crew quarters are actually meant for it. Crew quarters afford privacy and comfort in a domain where such things are luxuries. When your crew **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work\|Works]]** a side job that uses the ship, you can charge more.

### Reinforced Armored Plating

Reinforced metal armor that covers all the vulnerable parts of your ship. Grants **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Armor\|armor]]** against ship damage. Damage suffered by [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems\|ship systems]] does not affect that system's modules until the system is fully disabled.

### Exosuit Bay

A holding bay for one Magitech [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuit]] (not included), and all of the space and tools necessary to refuel, re-arm, refit, and repair your exosuit. Required to effectively store, deploy, repair, and re-arm an exosuit.

### Glider Bay

Ramp and holding bay for holding enough [[8. Glossary/Glider\|gliders]] for the crew.

### Smuggling Compartments

Enough space to carry a small shipment. These compartments are warded against visual, but not magical, inspections. At 3+ **hull** rating, these compartments are sufficient to accommodate smuggling people too.
