**Crew Quality** (also sometimes called **Crew Tier**) is a general measurement of your crew's talent, connections, wealth, and gear quality. It is the equivalent to a [[2. Faction Tier|faction's tier]] rating, except that while faction tier's go from 1 to 5, **Crew Quality** goes from 0 to 3.

The higher your **Crew Quality**, the more effectively you can take on higher tier factions. You can increase your Crew Quality by spending **2 [[6. Crew Advancement|crew advancements]]** and **[[Coin]]** equal to four times the new rating.
