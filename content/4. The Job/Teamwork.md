When you’re in position to help a teammate, tell the GM what benefit you’re hoping for and say what you do to help. The GM has final say on which benefit you get, then they tell you what’s required to do it. This is an extended application of the **[[1. How to Play/Devil's Bargain]]**.
### Benefits
- Improved **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect\|effect]]** or **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Position\|position]]**
- **Cover** for them: they don't have to face a **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Threat Roll\|threat]]**
- **Protect** them: reduce the severity of a **[[1. How to Play/Harm, Stress & Trauma/Consequences\|consequence]]**
- **+1d** to a roll
- More details or questions when they **[[1. How to Play/Gathering Information\|gather info]]**
- **1 (or more) ticks** on a **[[1. How to Play/The Basics/Progress Clocks\|clock]]**
### Requirements
- **None** (you simply do it)
- Face a **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Threat Roll\|threat]]**
- Suffer **[[1. How to Play/Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]]**
- Take **1 [[1. How to Play/Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]**
- **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Push Yourself]]**
- **1 (or more) ticks** on a **[[1. How to Play/The Basics/Progress Clocks\|clock]]**

## Group Actions & Scale
When the group faces the same threat (like everyone sneaking past a guard patrol), each player rolls the same action (like **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Skulk]]** in this case) to see how the consequence manifests for their character.

If the situation has multiple different threats, the team can split up and tackle them individually to address the problems in a variety of ways, each using whichever action is appropriate to what they’re doing.

> “Okay, while you Skulk past the sentries, I’ll Helm the ship into position (quietly!) so you can drop down with the loot. Maybe Ruby can Study the airship patrols and make sure we’re clear to escape?”

When a team combines their efforts, they’ll usually have more **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect\|effect]]** or a better **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Position\|position]]** because of an increase in **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect#Scale\|scale]]**. If all four crew members **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Scrap]]** as a team, for instance, they’re equal in scale to a guard squad, so no one faces worse [[1. How to Play/Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]] (and a [[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Position#Desperate\|desperate]] position) as they would if fighting alone.

## Leading a Team
If you’re designated as the team’s leader, you can **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Push Yourself\|push yourself]]** on behalf of anyone who’s acting with the team (in addition to the PC pushing themselves). Describe how your leadership helps them reduce or avoid the consequence.
