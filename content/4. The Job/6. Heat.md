Duradin is full of prying eyes and informants. Anything you do might be witnessed, and there’s always evidence left behind. To reflect this, your crew acquires **Heat** as they commit crimes. This represents the amount of pressure the local authorities can bring down on your character.

After a [[4. The Job/1. The Job\|job]] or conflict with an opponent, your crew takes **Heat** according to how the job played out:

| Heat         | Effect                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Base**     | **0** for quiet, low exposure or **2** for a standard job. **+1 Heat** per crew Tier.                            |
| **Target**   | **+1** to **+4** for a high profile or well-connected target.                                                    |
| **Chaos**    | **+1** for open combat. **+2** for destruction or mayhem. **+2** if you're at work with another faction.         |
| **Death**    | **+4** if death occurred in connection to the job.                                                               |
| **Evidence** | **+1** to **+4** for witnesses willing to talk or evidence left behind (depending on the quality and/or amount). |
### Reducing Heat
After the GM determines how much **Heat** is gained from a job, the players have a chance to reduce that amount. Players can choose to ask an appropriate **contact** or **[[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]]** to reduce Heat before applying it to the crew sheet. The more powerful or connected they are, the more they can reduce (**-1 to -4 Heat**). Friendly connection may do this for a few **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin\|coin]]** (**1 per Heat**), as a **favor**, or in exchange for a **service**. Other connections expect more in return (**2 coin per Heat** or a **significant service**).

### Gaining Heat
Once the players have accepted the heat gained from the job, you mark Heat for the [[8. Glossary/Khorvate\|region]] the job was performed in. Each [[8. Glossary/Khorvate\|region]] tracks **Heat** separately.

If a job spans multiple regions, place the **Heat** on the region in which the majority of the illegal activity happened. If it was loud and chaotic across multiple region, discuss the situation with your table. You may have picked up illegal cargo in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] but fought a freighter in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]]. In that case, maybe you put the **Heat** in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]], as that’s where the most “noise” of the job happened, or divide the **Heat** between both regions.

GMs: Whenever possible, don’t duplicate **Heat** (if you moved illegal goods from [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] through [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]] to [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]], all three systems should not get heat) unless major public events happened on each leg of the job.

# Wanted Level
When your **Heat** level reaches 8 in a [[8. Glossary/Khorvate\|region]], you gain a **Wanted Level** and clear your **Heat** (any excess **Heat** “rolls over,” so if your **Heat** was 7 and you took 4 **Heat**, you’d reset with 3 **Heat** marked).

The higher your **Wanted Level**, the more serious the response when law enforcement takes action against you (they’ll send a force of higher quality and scale).

Also, your **Wanted Level** contributes to the severity of the **[[5. Downtime/3. Entanglements\|entanglements]]** that your crew faces after a job.

When the crew gains a **Wanted Level**, mark **[[3. The Crew/6. Crew Advancement\|2 crew xp]]**.
### Reducing Wanted Level
The main way to reduce your **Wanted Level** is through jail time. When one of your crew members, friends, contacts — or a framed enemy — is convicted and incarcerated for crimes associated with your crew, your **Wanted Level** is reduced by 1 and you clear your **Heat**.

The severity of jail time depends on your **Wanted Level**:

- **Wanted Level 4:** Life sentence or execution
- **Wanted Level 3:** A year or two.
- **Wanted Level 2:** Several months.
- **Wanted Level 1:** A month or two.
- **Wanted Level 0:** A few weeks. Or the authorities give you a beating to teach you a lesson (suffer a level 3 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]], no resistance roll allowed).

Jail is dehumanizing and brutal. The reputation of your crew is your only real defense inside. When you serve time, roll on the table below using your [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]] as the dice pool.

- **Critical:** You make a name for yourself inside. Your crew gains **+1 faction status** with a [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] that you assisted while you served your time. You get out in half the time due to good behavior.
- **6:** You do your time well. Your crew gains **+1 faction status** with a [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] that you assisted while you served your time.
- **4/5:** You keep your head down and do your time without incident.
- **1-3:** It's horrific. You suffer a **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Trauma]]** from the experience.
