The Dwarven Republic of Duradin is an ancient homeland to much of dwarf-kind, one of the oldest. It is a place where government, industry, religion, and family are all intertwined. The territory that Duradin occupies is collectively called [[Khett]], or sometimes the Khettish Steppes or the Khettish Highlands. [[Khett]] is used more frequently in conversation when referring to the region.

# History

Prior to the current country of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]] the region was entirely controlled by Giants. [[Khett]], the name ancient dwarves gave to this land, was but a small part of their hemisphere-spanning kingdom of Ostoria. Of course one major factor in Ostorian society was the use of slaves, of whom Dwarves were the most common.

Around 1700 years ago marked the rapid rise of the human empire Nerath, which is regarded as one of the major factors contributing to the decline of the Giants' reign over the continent. Nerath's empire spread quickly and claimed long-held Ostorian territories, and with it the slaves held by the giants were repatriated into Nerathan society.

[[Khett]] was one of the last remaining bastions of Giant society (along with Iradrum in the north, and Skargos far to the west). Disconnected from each other, these independent small kingdoms did not last much longer. The dwarves of [[Khett]] were the first reclaim their freedom, followed by Skargos, and then Iradrum.

And so it was that the last bastions of Giant culture were also the birthplace of what might be considered the major Dwarven cultures.

# Government

As a republic, Duradin's governance is a complex mix of decentralized local governments and a highly centralized federal governmental core. Add to that the complex familial and clan structures that stretch through all layers of government, and you have a very complicated system.

At its most local level, government is often run by individual [[Clan|clans]]. Small clans run small villages, slightly larger clans might be responsible for a handful of villages and might have a moderate presence at the nearest large town.

The pattern generally holds that the larger a [[Clan|clan]] is, the larger the territory it controls. This owes quite simply to the fact that more people means more labor and income. Often smaller clans will band together and collectively control larger regions, or larger clans will bequeath territory to smaller clans in exchange for fealty.

# Politics

For the last 60 years the dwarves have been going through an industrial revolution, developing wondrous technological machines and tools, the most wide-spread of which is the [[Airship|airship]]. Currently, dwarves are split into 2 political groups:

- Iron Dwarves – Are the current majority right now. They believe that their future is through the development of magitech machines.
- Earthen Dwarves – Opposite of the Iron Dwarves. They hold that the current use of magitech is tarnishing the proud tradition of dwarves, and believe in the primal traditions of their culture.

Political tension between these two groups has risen a lot in the recent years, following several anti-industrial attacks. Many are worried that a civil war may break out between the two sides.
