#### Rule

[[Planning & Engagement#Engagement Roll|Engagement rolls]] in Nòm Råsh are always at **-1d** due to ever-present security forces patrolling the skies. Any jobs run against Guilders here are considered to be on hostile turf.

## Description

A stronghold for [[Guild of Engineers|Guild]] activity, the mountains and hills of Nòm Råsh are dotted with mines and quarries. Nevermind that many of them are worked by indentured or imprisoned dwarves, and definitely don't question why they're there in the first place.

## Scene

Rhythmic sounds of pickaxes in an open quarry. A prison guard watches over the worker housing block in a mining camp. A watchtower surveys the surrounding hills while airships sail overhead.

## NPCs

#### Rog Bloodhammer

Guilder head of operations here. Known for getting things done, even if it means bending the rules. A thrill-seeker, he keeps a small racing airship for rare days off. Commanding, shrewd, bold.

#### Krin Kas

A druid who claims to hear the voice of [[Natural Spirits#Stone's Unyielding Endurance|Stone's Unyielding Endurance]]. Trades information, including esoterica on ancient and lost relics. Passionate, strange, religious.

#### Lokum "Bolt" Boulderbelly

Labor organizer for the miners. Rabble-rouser beloved by the workers. Has been incarcerated numerous times for for crimes both real and fabricated. Popular, dissident, ambitious.
