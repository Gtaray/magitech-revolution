#### Rule
Clan conflicts in Etar Nökor are commonplace, but by [[8. Glossary/Magnar]]’s iron decree murder is met with the harshest punishment. Those needing to settle blood feuds resort to stealing, kidnapping, and ransoming. Deaths on a [[The Job\|job]] generate **+8 [[Heat]]** (instead of +4).

## Description
A picturesque landscape of rolling hills covered in a sea of grasses that wave in the breeze. There are few settlements large enough to call a city; most live a humble life off their lot of land. Lawful structures are lax here; a perk to most, but it means that clan disputes are rampant.

The lack of "civilization" means wild beasts and criminals run amok, and the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]] stamps out those problems with an iron fist.

## Scene
Quick bets taken on an open brawl between two clans over slights. A log cabin at the edge of an orchard, tended by a family. A Bloodhammer hunting party tracks a while manticore. A crossroads, where iron cages hanging from posts hold the corpses of cut-rate criminals.

## NPCs
#### Ulrich Bloodhammer
[[8. Glossary/Magnar]] of the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]]. Tough and violent. He enforces the iron law on all those in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]]. Once a solder in the royal guard. Proud, demanding, honorable.
#### Zek Copperbottle
Merchant always willing to make a deal. His beard is festooned with gold and silver jewelry and tell the story of his several-hundred-year life. Experienced, shrewd, loves to barter.
#### "Picks"
Mid-level [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]] boss. Arranges protection for smuggling runs and meets with potential clients. Cautious, charming, confident.
