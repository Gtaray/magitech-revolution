#### Rule

Everything is available on Dåtan Tor for a price. [[Acquire|Acquiring]] costs **+1 [[Coin]]** but is tracked with a **6-segment supply clock** (instead of a 4-segment clock).

## Description

Dozens of cities surrounding a large lake. The foothills and mountains north of the region are rich in iron, which is sluiced down to the lake and extracted from the sands. Many of the forests and woodlands in the region have been forested and replaced with farmland and workshops. An industrial place, made for hard-working dwarves. A primary hub for trade, meaning all sorts of goods come through here, meaning it's a prime destination for smugglers and fences alike.

## Scene

Lightning storms crack upon the hills while a summer monsoon swells the lake. Industrial workers dredge the shores of the lake. Cloaked strangers congregating around a steel warehouse before an auction opens. Row after row of airships grounded in open water.

## Notable NPCs

#### "Hex".

Outlaw and gunslinger. Has some obvious cybernetics from his [[Guild of Engineers|Guild]] days. Wanted in several [[Khorvate|khorvates]]. Runs a fighting ring in the mountains. Ruthless, fast cautious.

#### "Drake"

Fixer for hire and autctioneer. If she can't get it or sell it, she knows someone who can. Connected, confident, bold.

#### "Tank"

Mercenary turned [[Sundrop]] farmer. Knows countless hiding spots in the hills and mountains of the surrounding area. Gambler, commanding, experienced.

#### "Ash"

Sun-smoking, grizzled ex-aeronaut of the [[21st Squadron]]. Runs the Three Suns, a gambling den and the biggest local dive. Deadly, retired, steely.
