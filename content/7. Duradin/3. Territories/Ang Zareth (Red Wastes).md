#### Rule
Nobody comes here who doesn’t have to. The desert hates you and jobs are hard to find. Even bounty hunters pass it by. **-1 [[Heat]]** for jobs in this territory.

## Description
A desert wasteland with little life and and less water. Only the hardiest, deadliest, or most cunning of creatures survive the merciless heat, which makes traveling the desert floor endlessly dangerous. The most dangerous of all are the [[8. Glossary/Sandwyrm\|sandwyrms]] that skate the skies and burrow under the sands. Despite this, the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]] cuts straight north-south through the deserts.

## Scene
Heavily armed guards patrolling the walls around a [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]] yard. Research crews packing for their next expedition across from treasure hunters doing the same. A smuggler discussing arrangements with a client in a dingy dive while the bartender makes them drinks.

A hunter mounting an expedition to catch the deadly Sandkheg for a rich client. Callous, experienced, gambler.

[[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Heartfire Clan]] member dealing in military-grade magitech. Cautious, meticulous, dissident

Magitech-enhanced mercenary. Travels with a [[8. Glossary/Spark]], an ex-priest, and an orc. Recently lost a party member and hops to replace them. Religious, vigilant, honorable.
#### Asha Runemantle
High ranking [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Runemantle Clan]] member, responsible for [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]] schedules. Occasionally willing to make backroom deals. Tired, jaded, relentless.
