#### Rule
The factories and mines have caused lingering air pollution; anyone spending time outside without protection gains level 1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]] "Ashen Lung."

## Description
A sizeable portion of magitech components manufactured in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] come from this industrialized region. Thick, rust-colored clouds create dusk even during the day. Factories line the walls of deep canyons and valleys, creating a truly dismal place to be.

## Scene
Dark and dirty canyons from which the forges of industry belch black smoke. Masked workers walking along metal platforms mounted to canyon walls. Workers use cranes to load large airship components onto a lightning rail car.

## NPCs
#### Locke Goldengut
Owns a number of discrete, well-guarded, storage spaces and maintains a strict no-questions policy. If it's illegal, it's certainly stored by Locke. Upstanding, wealthy, confident.
#### Kilm Shatterfist
Magitech dealer known for prototypes and specialty armaments. Recently acquired military schematics and plans to auction it under the cover of a storm. Connected, meticulous, careful.
#### "Roost"
Local [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]] head. Known for a brutal approach to criminal organization. Wears custom-tailored suits that hide extensive magitech gear. Fierce, ruthless, demanding.
