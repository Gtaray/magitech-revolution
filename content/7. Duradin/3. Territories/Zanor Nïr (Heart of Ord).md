#### Rule

Zanor Nïr is the economic heart and seat of power in Duradin. Working a side job here **pays more**, but generates **2 [[Heat]]**.

## Description

Zanor Nïr is home to the capital of Duradin, the ancient city of [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Baern]]. It is the economic and political heart of the country, which means that all types, from the wealthiest nobles and businessdwarves to the poorest, down-on-their-luck commoners, are found here. It is a place of highly stratified society, guarded closely by the [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Ironroot Clan]] and their allies. You can find anything here... for a price.

## Scene

A bustling city street carved into the face of a mountain with airships slowly moving in and out of the skydock. Airships streaming between towering spires. The beat of street musicians playing on the corner of a dark canyon-city. Socialites attending a gala at the Magnar's mansion.

[[8. Glossary/Magnar]] of the [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Anvilhelm Clan]], and close friend to [[8. Glossary/Thane]] Hruun Ironroot IV, and major player in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Ramthas]]. Political, stern, connected.

#### Rocco Skyson

Ship designer extraordinaire. Only makes one of each ship designed. Artistic, brilliant, aloof.

#### Usan Zulban

Once feared assassin among the [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]], now a cunning syndicate leader. Loves good food and pit fights. Sly, corpulent, sartorial, decadent.
