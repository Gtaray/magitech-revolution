#### Rule

Zanor Nïr is the economic heart and seat of power in Duradin. Working a side job here **pays more**, but generates **2 [[Heat]]**.

## Description

Zanor Nïr is home to the capital of Duradin, the ancient city of [[Baern]]. It is the economic and political heart of the country, which means that all types, from the wealthiest nobles and businessdwarves to the poorest, down-on-their-luck commoners, are found here. It is a place of highly stratified society, guarded closely by the [[Ironroot Clan]] and their allies. You can find anything here... for a price.

## Scene

A bustling city street carved into the face of a mountain with airships slowly moving in and out of the skydock. Airships streaming between towering spires. The beat of street musicians playing on the corner of a dark canyon-city. Socialites attending a gala at the Magnar's mansion.

## Notable NPCs

#### Magnar Obok Anvilhelm

[[Magnar]] of the [[Anvilhelm Clan]], and close friend to [[Thane]] Hruun Ironroot IV, and major player in [[Ramthas]]. Political, stern, connected.

Owner and operator of the Lock Luna, the most infamous bar in the Ramthan undercity. Cunning, unforgiving, popular

Ship designer extraordinaire. Only makes one of each ship designed. Artistic, brilliant, aloof.

#### Usan Zulban

Once feared assassin among the [[Ashen Knives]], now a cunning syndicate leader. Loves good food and pit fights. Sly, corpulent, sartorial, decadent.
