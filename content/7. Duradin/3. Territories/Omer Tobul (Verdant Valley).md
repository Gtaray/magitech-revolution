#### Rule

Omer Tobul is full of money, but is a Guild stronghold and thus closely monitored. When you do a job in Omer Tobul, you get **+1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** and **[[+1 Heat]]**

## Description

Known as "The Green" throughout [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], this valley is lush with woodlands compared to the deserts of eastern [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. The surrounding mountains also make it fairly isolated, with only a few common routes in and out, making it quite easy for the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]] and [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] to control what moves through the region. It is a stronghold for industrialists, and the birthplace of modern magitech engineering.

## NPCs

#### Rukh and Rin

Most feared bounty hunter in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Rin, his golden eagle partner, is a known killer. They are on retainer to hunt any criminals who poke their nose in [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]] business that's not theirs. Ruthless, vigilant, commanding.

#### Urvad Anvilhelm

High-ranking logistics officer in the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], responsible for all shipments in and out of Omer Tobul. Privy to much of the Guild's supply structure. Popular, demanding, shrewd.

#### Jyri Greenvein

Jeweler known for incorporating magical enchantments into her work. Her creations may be the most artful pieces in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. A Jyri piece can open doors in the most elite circles. Artistic, charming, proud.
