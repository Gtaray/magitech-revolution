#### Rule

## Description
The most temperate and hospitable of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann\|Oghranni]] territories, the majority of folk live in this region. While the people are generally distrusting of outsiders, their strong sense of decorum and kindness means that even strangers are met with hospitality. The druids here tend to their flowers to produce all manner of medicine.

## Scene
Massive, person-sized flowers blooming along a vine-supported path through the trees. The sweet smell of honey floating through the air. Druids and mystics gather inside a stone henge and perform a ritual. Sick travelers pray while waiting outside a healer's temple.

#### Urist Steelson
A noble from the inner clans. Seeking to learn from the druids so that he can cultivate a plant for a new brewery he's planning to grow in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]]. Armed, deadly, observant.
