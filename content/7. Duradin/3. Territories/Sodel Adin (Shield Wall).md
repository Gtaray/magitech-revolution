#### Rule

Although only Guilders are technically allowed to use the Khalud Academy archives and workshops, any **[[Work#Long-Term Project|long-term projects]]** to **[[Study]]** schematics fill **+1 tick**.

## Description

A region that is ravaged by frequent lightning storms that occasionally strike sand and rock to produce unique minerals essential to magitech production. There is a substantial civilian population, including Duradin's preeminent educational institution, [[Khalud Academy]].

## Scene

Professors walking down the polished stone paths of the Academy. A market street with insistent vendors selling kebabs to hungry miners. Excavators packing furiously onto skiffs, ready to take advantage of a recent lightning storm. The storm alert blaring citywide.

## NPCs

#### Nish Grimnail

An independent investigator looking for evidence of a conspiracy at [[Khalud Academy]], where several top students have vanished. Ambitious, vigilant, charming.

#### Edzul Amberspire

[[Guild of Engineers|Guild]] Engineer that works on the electrostatic dispersion array that keeps the storms away from the university. Collects colored glass statues. Dedicated, brilliant, overworked.

#### Athel Saltstone

Minerals trader with a load of stolen goods she needs to move. In hiding after her first deal went wrong. Scared, cunning, proud.

#### Sahar

Orc shaman that lives in the foothills. Odd, blue-eyed, insightful.
