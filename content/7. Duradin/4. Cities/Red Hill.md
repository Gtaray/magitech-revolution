Red Hill is a city built into the face of the massive red mountains that split northern and southern Duradin.

Not just the largest city in [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Ibruk Lun (Sleeping Stone)]], but the largest city in all of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]]. It's location is basically in the direct center of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], which makes it a very valuable trade hub, and is why the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] has such a strong presence in this city. The [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]] have outsource most of the governance of this city to the Guild and their allies.
