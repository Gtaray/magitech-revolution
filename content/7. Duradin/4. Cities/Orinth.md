Carved into the walls of a canyon, Orith is home to the train yards that maintain the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]], a magitech levitating train that delivers goods at high speed to and from remote parts of the country. Goods that arrive here are delivered to their final destination, via airship if the destination allows for it, or by cart or wagon if going deeper into the mountains.

The city avoids the heat of the desert by being carved into the faces of canyon walls, while the rail itself runs along the floor of the canyon. The canyons are large enough for small airships to navigate safely and medium ships to navigate carefully, but large ships simply cannot fit.

Orith is a major distribution hub for the hundreds of small towns deep underground. It is managed by the the [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Runemantle Clan]], who also own and operate the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]].
