A town built on the shores of Lake Ulm in [[Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]], [[Durvos]]. Kar Darum is a terminus for the [[Lightning Rail]], which makes it a very busy town. It is occupied by most of the local clans and guilds, including the [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Runemantle Clan]], [[Cobalt Syndicate]], [[Red Sash]], [[Amberspire Clan]], and more.
