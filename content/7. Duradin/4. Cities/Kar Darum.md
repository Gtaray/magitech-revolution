A town built on the shores of Lake Ulm in [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]], [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]]. Kar Darum is a terminus for the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]], which makes it a very busy town. It is occupied by most of the local clans and guilds, including the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/7. Other Dwarf Clans/Runemantle Clan]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Red Sash]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]], and more.
