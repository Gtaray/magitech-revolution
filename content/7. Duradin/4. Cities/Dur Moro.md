The largest city in [[Dåtan Tor (Iron Shores)]], built on the northern shores of Rustwaters. It serves as a major transit hub for the [[Lightning Rail]] as well as a common rest stop for skyships, as the lake afford ample room for ships to [[Airship#Grounding|ground]].

The city is governed by the [[Amberspire Clan]], though the [[Ashen Knives|Ashen Knives']] influence often surpasses the [[Khorvar|khorvar's]] when it comes to local ordinance.
