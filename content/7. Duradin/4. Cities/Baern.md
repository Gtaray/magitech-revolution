The capital city of all of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]], and the native home of the [[Ironroot Clan]]. While it is governed by the [[Ironroot Clan]], it is a neutral ground to all dwarves, and it is forbidden to draw a weapon while in the bounds of the city.

Baern is where the [[Grand Council]] meets and deliberates, and it is where [[Thane]] Hruun Ironroot IV spends most of his time, running the country.
