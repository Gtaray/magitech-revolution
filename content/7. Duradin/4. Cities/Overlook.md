A fortified keep overlooking Lake Mead in central [[Etar Nökor (King's Crest)]], [[Torhmek]]. While from the outside it looks like a single castle with a few outlying houses, it is in fact an entire carved deep into the hill upon which the keep stands. The town's unique structure is due to it's original purpose: to watch for wyrms, wyverns, and dragons that might fly down from the mountains.
