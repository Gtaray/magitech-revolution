The only town found in the deserts of [[Ang Zareth (Red Wastes)]], cut from the sides of a deep river canyon. The [[Lightning Rail|Lightning Rail's]] route passes by here, though it rarely stops at the town.

The city itself is not a particular safe place to be; organized crime is said to run most of the city. But if you keep your head down and mind your manners, it is a quiet place to to hide.
