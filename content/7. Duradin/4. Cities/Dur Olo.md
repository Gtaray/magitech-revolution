One of the largest and wealthiest cities in the country. Located in southern [[Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]], [[Durvos]], Dur Olo is home to the [[Guild of Engineers]] headquarters, as well as seat of power for the [[Amberspire Clan]]. The city is well protected, both by the governors and the organized crime families that operate here. They have a well-balanced system, and any disruption to that is handled quickly and harshly.
