One of the largest and wealthiest cities in the country. Located in southern [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]], [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]], Dur Olo is home to the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] headquarters, as well as seat of power for the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]]. The city is well protected, both by the governors and the organized crime families that operate here. They have a well-balanced system, and any disruption to that is handled quickly and harshly.
