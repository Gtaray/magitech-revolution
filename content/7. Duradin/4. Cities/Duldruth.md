A major trading hub in the southern reaches of [[Egar Lûrit (Old Spine)]]. This city sits atop the high hills of the Old Spine, and is the first big city one would encounter on the road from Arran'ak to Duradin.

Tall skydocks reach upwards out of the city's thick tangle of carved buildings, while underground tunnels wind beneath the mountain stone.
