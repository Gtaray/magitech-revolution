Deep, deep underground is the city of Hrakhammar. It was the capital of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]] in the past, though now is a mostly known for its massive foundries, as well as the embarkation point for the most well-traveled route to the Underdark. The [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Deepforge Clan]] runs Hrakhammar as well most of its foundries.

The [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Deepforge Clan]] are the only ones that know the secret of smelting and purifying adamantine in its raw form, and have the only mines from which adamantine is extracted in any meaningful amount.
