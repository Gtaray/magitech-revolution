The highest security prison in [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]], operated by [[Deeprock]]. Located far to the north near [[Siege Rock]] in [[Ibruk Lun (Sleeping Stone)]].

The majority of the prison is below ground, carved into the mountains. Many have tried to escape, but only two people have ever made it out alive.
