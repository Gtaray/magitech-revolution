The City of Gold Stone. Harheldur is nestled amidst the north-western mountains of [[Irol Zimkel (Northern Plains)]]. The stone the city is built from is veined with orange agate, and the city is architected so that during summer, the light of the setting sun causes the agate to glow like molten gold.
