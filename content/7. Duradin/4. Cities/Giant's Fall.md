The [[Bloodhammer Clan|Bloodhammer Clan's]] seat of power. The city is built out of, and around, the petrified remains of an enormous wyrm that, legends say, was slain by a Bloodhammer [[Gods#Heroes|Hero]]. It is said that during the battle, the blood of this ancient wyrm stained the mountains red and boiled the rivers and lakes, turning [[Khett]] into the desert it is now.

The city is a rowdy place, and rarely is justice meted out by the governors, leaving it to the people to sort out their squabbles.
