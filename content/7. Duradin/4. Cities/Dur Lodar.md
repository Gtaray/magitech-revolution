A border fortress between [[Dåtan Tor (Iron Shores)]] and [[Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]] in [[Durvos]]. It is highly fortified and defended by the [[Guild of Engineers]] and the [[Amberspire Clan]], as it is a major checkpoint for the [[Lightning Rail]].

Airships into [[Omer Tobul (Verdant Valley)]] must check in with the [[Sender's Guild]] stationed here, meaning they (and by extension the [[Guild of Engineers]]) are aware of all ships in their skies.
