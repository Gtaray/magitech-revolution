The homeland for modern magitech, Durvos is where the most brilliant minds conjure up a better tomorrow. Its lake valleys and rocky forests support a wide array of life here, from large urbanized cities to small homesteads on the frontier. 

Durvos is a center for innovation; the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] HQ is located here, and they have a strong hand in much of what goes on. The [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]] boasts some of the best alchemists and enchanters in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Together the two have pushed magitech unrelentingly forward.

## Notable Places
#### [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Dur Olo]]
The seat of power for the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]], and the home to the headquarters of the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]]. The city proper sits on the shores of a large lake, but continues to the surrounding mountains where it's carved into the cliffsides. Few know how deep into cliffside the caverns go, or what secret workshops and laboratories are hidden in there.
#### Shipyards
While the primary yard is run by the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], many smaller, licensed hubs work on repairs and ship refits. These shops are full of bored flyboys looking for any distraction from the wait. Some foolhardy among the bunch are hired for prototype tests.
#### [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Khalud Academy]]
Originally a public institute, the academy was purchased by the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] two decades ago. Since then it has been the premiere place for technical learning in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], though the cost for attending is prohibitive for most. While only guilders and students are allowed to use the facilities, it's common enough to get a day pass if you know someone.
#### Jenk's Junkyard
A massive junkyard where decommissioned magitech is scrapped and sold for parts. If you're looking for equipment on the cheap, the Junkyard is your place, though it will likely be missing a piece or unreliable. Jenk also buys, but is a canny haggler.
