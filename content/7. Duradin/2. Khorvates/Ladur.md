The surface of Ladur is largely a rocky desert wasteland dotted with tall plateaus. Mountains border it on nearly every side, and it's in these mountains where nearly all Laduran dwarves live, deep underground. When outsiders picture dwarves, it's usually these guys they think of.

Underground cities in Ladur are sprawling and massive cave networks interspersed with grand halls. Cities above ground are nestled high up in the mountains where the temperatures are more tolerable, carved into the rockface of the cliffs.

Open-air cities play an important role in Ladur; they're the hubs through which airship trade happens. Travel on the surface is dangerous; not just the climate, but the plethora of desert-dwelling creatures that would gladly hunt a dwarf for their next meal.

## Notable Places

#### [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Ramthas]]

The largest open-air city built into nearly the entirety of the top of one of the tallest mountain in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] (also named Ramthas). Massive columns have been carved out over hundreds of years to hold up the mountain's peak, and on these columns the city is built, layer by layer, as the city expands. It is the most populated city in all of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], the largest center of commerce, and the capital of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]]. It also contains the headquarters for several factions, including the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Sender's Guild]], and [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Dragonshield Clan]]

#### [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Orinth]]

Carved into the walls of a canyon, Orinth is home to the train yards that maintain the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]], a magitech levitating train that delivers goods at high speed to and from remote parts of the country. Goods that arrive here are delivered to their final destination, via airship if the destination allows for it, or by cart or wagon if going deeper into the mountains. The city avoids the heat of the desert by being carved into the faces of canyon walls, while the rail itself runs along the floor of the canyon. The canyons are large enough for small [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]] to navigate safely and medium ships to navigate carefully, but large ships simply cannot fit.

#### The Straylight

A upscale, extra-dimensional, club and bar where elites can wine and dine. It has entrances in several of the main cities, though no one knows where the actual club itself is located. It's owner, Chance, runs a right establishment, but things can sometimes get out of hand.

#### Lor Gate

Mysterious ruins in the desert that contain a stone henge carved with sigils. It's unknown who built the ruins but study has revealed the henge is an ancient teleportation circle. Expeditions to the Lor Gate are difficult, as its location changes over time; it has been found in at least a dozen different places.
