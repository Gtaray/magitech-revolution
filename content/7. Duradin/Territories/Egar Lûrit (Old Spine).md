#### Rule
Egar Lûrit has some of the oldest cities in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] and boasts rich and varied cultures. Life is about who you know. Any [[3. The Crew/Crew Quality]] rolls use **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Consort]]** instead.

## Description
The western mountain range in [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Ladur]] that contains some of the oldest dwarven settlements in Duradin. Rich and diverse cultures that have weathered the thousands of years the dwarves have lived in the region.

## Scene
Ancient caverns beneath the mountains that hide untold treasures and dangers. A temple carved and crafted over centuries with groups of silent pilgrims entering and exiting. A celebration thrown in a grand feasting hall underground. A small settlement nestled in a mountain valley.

## Notable NPCs
#### Saren Galia
Information broker and bookie. When you can't pay your debts, you become her informant. Paranoid, fast, connected.
#### Lota
Fashionista and taste-maker, dressed in elaborate costumes. Secret a high-powered fixer. Has been known to take charity cases when the cause appeals to her. Popular, passionate, meticulous.
#### 'Jet'
[[6. Factions/Criminal/Green Dragons]] assassin. Can be hired for the right price, but only takes jobs in other territories. Travels with a large blue drakehound. Aloof, confident, unforgiving.
#### Ustal Sunblood
Best lawyer in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Cunning, connected, expensive.
