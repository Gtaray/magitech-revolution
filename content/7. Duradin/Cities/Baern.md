The capital city of all of [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], and the native home of the [[6. Factions/Other Dwarf Clans/Ironroot Clan]]. While it is governed by the [[6. Factions/Other Dwarf Clans/Ironroot Clan]], it is a neutral ground to all dwarves, and it is forbidden to draw a weapon while in the bounds of the city.

Baern is where the [[8. Glossary/Grand Council]] meets and deliberates, and it is where [[8. Glossary/Thane]] Hruun Ironroot IV spends most of his time, running the country.
