We use **Coin** as an abstract measure of cash and liquid assets. Coin includes coins officially minted by the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]] (the standard currency in Duradin), jewels, billets of precious metals, antique treasures, and any other valuable that is easily moved.

The few nominal coins PCs use in their daily lives are not tracked. If a character wants to toss a few coins around to achieve a small goal (bribe a doorman), use the PC’s [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Stash#Lifestyle Rating\|lifestyle rating]] for a [[1. How to Play/6. Fortune Roll\|fortune roll]].

## Monetary Values

- **1 Coin:** A small purse of minted coins. A month's laborer's wages.
- **2 Coin:** A fine weapon. Several months' laborer's wages.
- **3 Coin:** A weekly income for a small business. A fine piece of art. A set of luxury clothes.
- **4 Coin:** An exquisite jewel. An advanced piece of magitech.
- **5 Coin:** A good monthly take for a small business. A safe full of rare metals and valuables. A rare artifact.
- **6 Coin:** A powerful magical item. An airship module.
- **8 Coin:** Monthly income for a moderate business.
- **10 Coin:** A simple home. An exosuit.
- **20 Coin:** A small airship. A treasure trove.
- **30 Coin:** A modest home, small business
- **40 Coin:** A well appointed home, complete with servants and wait-staff. Enough to live a life of luxury without working a day.

More than **4 Coin** is an impractical amount to keep unnoticed. You must spend the excess or put it in your **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Stash]]**. A crew can also store **4 Coin** in their ship, by default. Any **Coin** beyond their limit must be spent as soon as possible (typically before the next job) or be distributed among the crew members.

## Coin Use

- Spend **1 Coin** to get an additional [[5. Downtime/1. Downtime\|activity]] during [[5. Downtime/1. Downtime\|downtime]].
- Spend **1 Coin** to increase the result level of a [[5. Downtime/1. Downtime\|downtime]] activity.
- Spend **Coin** to avoid certain crew [[5. Downtime/3. Entanglements\|entanglements]].
- Put **Coin** in your character’s [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Stash]] (1-for-1) to improve their [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Stash#Lifestyle Rating\|lifestyle]] and circumstances when they retire.
- Spend **Coin** when you advance your [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|crew quality]].

# Debt

You may go into **debt** with a provider if they agree. Note the initial coin you owe, then make a 4-segment clock and tick it every downtime you still owe the debt. if the clock fills, the provider takes steps to get their money back. To remove a tick, pay a coin equal to half the initial amount (rounded up) or do a favor. To resolve your debt, pay the initial amount when you have no ticks on the clock.
