You are the crew’s speed demon and "get out of trouble" card. When the chips are down and the [[6. Factions/Industrialists/21st Squadron\|Squadron]] is on your tail, there’s nobody else they’d rather turn to. There’s no [[8. Glossary/Airship\|skyship]] you can’t overdrive, no canyon or storm you can’t navigate through. You might be a hot-rodding show-off or calm and cool behind the helm. You might take desperate risks or thrive when everything is on the line. But you’re always ahead of the pack.

**When you play a Pilot, you earn xp when you address challenges with speed or flair.** Fire a warning shot or do a barrel roll. It’s your show, live it.

*How’d you get so good with skyships? What’s the most risky stunt you’ve pulled off—and who knows about it? How did you get a homunculus? Are you fearless, or just very, very good?*
# Playing a Pilot
Those who spend their lives flying [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airships]] across the skies of [[8. Glossary/Khett]], running from pirates and government forces alike, have a need for speed. You have a unique ability to push into and past danger, relying on your skill and your [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airships]] to come through in the end. Whereas the [[2. The Characters/Playbooks/Mechanic]] may be what keeps the ship in shape, you’re what gives the ship purpose. More than a few bar-room wanted posters have been about a hotshot pilot and their [[8. Glossary/Airship\|skyship]].

As the Pilot, you’ll be of central focus when the ship needs to be maneuvered on the job. But **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Helm]]** is not confined to ships alone. [[8. Glossary/Glider\|Gliders]], [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuits]], and riding animals being ridden can be **Helmed** just as well. Whatever your getaway vehicle, **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Helm]]** has you covered.

Where did you pick up your piloting skills? Did you fight for the [[6. Factions/Industrialists/21st Squadron\|Squadron]] but ran into trouble with the law? Have you grown up amongst racers and always loved the spotlight? Perhaps you quit a respectable job hauling cargo for a bit more excitement. Remember to bring those influences into your descriptions of piloting. Talk about the crisp military maneuvers you use to shake a brigand or the way you drift into the wind in order to correct a hard turn.

While **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Helm]]** handles most piloting actions, consider how you might move fast in other ways. Perhaps **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Scramble]]** when you’re on foot, and **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Sway]]** for fast-talking.

For Veteran abilities, consider picking up the [[2. The Characters/Playbooks/Mechanic\|Mechanic's]] [[2. The Characters/Playbooks/Mechanic#Overcharge\|Overcharge]] ability to keep your ship safe in fights. For total speed-demons, look at the [[2. The Characters/Playbooks/Scoundrel\|Scoundrel's]] [[2. The Characters/Playbooks/Scoundrel#Daredevil\|Daredevil]] ability.

# Special Abilities
## Starting Ability
### Ace Pilot
You have **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect#Potency\|potency]]** on all speed-related rolls. When you roll to **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Push Yourself#Resistance Roll\|resist]]** the consequences of piloting, gain **+1d**.

*Your potency might be balanced out by their better quality (if their ship engines are better than yours, for instance). Remember that the consequences of piloting are not always ship damage but can often be resisted just the same.*

## Special Abilities
### Commander
Whenever you lead a [[4. The Job/Teamwork#Group Actions & Scale\|group action]], gain **+1 [[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect#Scale\|scale]]** (for example, a small group counts as a medium group). If you [[4. The Job/Teamwork#Leading a Team\|lead]] a group action in combat, you may count multiple **6s** from different rolls as a **critical.**

*A group action is one where multiple PCs perform the same action and roll their respective pools for those actions. If you already have scale on your opponent, you gain an additional scale (and therefore level of effect). If two or more players roll a **6**, then the result of the roll for everyone is a **crit**. You must still suffer stress costs from any participant whose result is a **1-3**.*

### Eye in the Sky
When you **[[4. The Job/Teamwork\|assist]]** a crewmate while onboard an [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]], you can spend your **[[1. How to Play/Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to ignore any cost you pay or consequence you suffer for that action.

*This ability is not limited to only piloting. This works for any assistance you provide a crewmate while you are onboard any airship (not just your own). Since this ignores all costs and consequences, think big.*

### Hedonist
When you [[5. Downtime/Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice\|induldge]] your **vice**, you roll 2 fewer dice. One ally who joins you may do the same.

*Any ally must join you in whatever vice you’re indulging. This reduces the likelihood of overindulging.*

### Keen Eye
You have sharp eyes and notice small details many might overlook. Gain **+1d** when firing ship weapons or making trick shots.

*This can be used as a basis for resistance rolls to act first. “No, I want to act before him—I would’ve seen him reaching for the gun.” Also, trick shots can be performed with almost any kind of ranged weapon, not just ship weapons.*

### Leaf of the Wind
When you **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Push Yourself\|push yourself]]** to **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Push Yourself#Resistance Roll\|resist]]** a threat, you may spend **+1 [[1. How to Play/Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]** to increase the **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect\|effect]]** of the **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Threat Roll]]**.

*Normally it takes two separate pushes to achieve the same effect. This can be used on any roll.*

### Adrenaline Junkie
When you make a [[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Threat Roll\|threat roll]] facing 3+ **threats**, mark **1 [[2. The Characters/Character Advancement\|xp]]**. You can spend your **[[1. How to Play/Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to reroll all of your dice that did not roll a **6**.

*The first ability is an incentive to really give in to your crazy ideas, even if they are likely to blow up in your face. The second ability helps avoid some of those truly terrible rolls.*

### Side Job
When **[[5. Downtime/Downtime Activities/Work\|Working]]** a side job, you can always charge more. When you do, you can learn rumors in the area.

*There may not be any rumors floating about, but the GM should treat this as a gather information roll, where you roll a 6 to learn about whatever is in the news and gossip at that port*

### Traveler
You’re comfortable around people of all cultures, especially those who might otherwise see you as an outsider. You gain **[[1. How to Play/Threat Roll/Effect#Potency\|potency]]** when attempting to **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Consort]]** with or **[[2. The Characters/Actions/Sway]]** them.

*While any character can have had some experience with specific foreign cultures, this ability represents a wide swath of experience and understanding. Whether you come across as deeply respectful or you just know what buttons to push, you are more effective than would be expected.*

# Pilot Items
### Grappling Hook
Small, but mechanized. Can pull you up. Fits in your belt. **\[1 Load\]**

*Makes you look dashing when you swing to the rescue. When did it last break?* 

### Guild License
A simple weapon (often a cudgel or walking stick) that has been masterfully worked. In the age of high magic and firearms, this might seem antiquated, but it is a humble, honest weapon. **\[1 Load\]**

*Who made this weapon? What training have you had with it? Does it hold sentimental value?*

### [[2. The Characters/Rules/Loadout#Quality\|Masterwork]] Homunculus
A small homunculus that supports piloting and can carry a few items. Seems eerily sentient, though cannot communicate in a traditional sense. **\[2 Load\]**

*Homunculi are supposed to have their personalities wiped about once a year. How long has it been since you've had yours taken to the Guild for such a procedure? What is its designation and what personality quirks does it have? What does it look like, and how does it help you pilot?*

### [[2. The Characters/Rules/Loadout#Quality\|Masterwork]] Jumpsuit
Sweet decals, emergency beacon, glide wings. Helps you survive getting shot down. Lets you move about a bit while falling and maybe even extends jumps from great heights. **\[1 Load\]**

*What patches or custom artwork do you have on yours, and what does it mean?*

### [[2. The Characters/Rules/Loadout#Quality\|Masterwork]] Mechanics Kit
Hand-held scanners, hull patch kit, assortment of hand tools. Everything you need to patch a vehicle and get it flying again. **\[1 Load\]**

*What tool did you just pick up? Which one did you customize?*

### Victory Cigars
Enough to share with a few choice people. What’s the point of pulling off amazing stunts if you can’t celebrate? No guarantee the rest of your crew will appreciate the smell. **\[0 Load\]**

*Where do you get these, and how many do you have left? How rare are they?*
