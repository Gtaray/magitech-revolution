Each player keeps track of the experience points (**xp**) that their character earns.

During the game session, mark xp:

- When you make a **desperate action roll**. You also gain this when you roll for a **group action** that’s desperate.

At the end of a session, for each of the below triggers, mark **1 xp** if it happened once, or **2 xp** if it happened multiple times.

- You invoke your character's [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice\|Vice]] or **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress#Invoking Trauma\|Trauma]]** to create a problem or complication.
- You or the GM invokes your character's **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|Harm]]** or **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm#Surviving Fatal Harm\|Scars]]** to create a problem or complication.
- When you **express your beliefs, drives, heritage, or background.** It's up to you how and when these manifest.
- When you meet your **playbook-specific xp trigger.** Each playbook describes ways that role tends to approach problems. You can mark xp even if the approach was unsuccessful.

# Advancement Clock

All characters have a **6-segment advancement clock**. Whenever you **mark xp**, tick the advancement clock. When the clock fills, it resets back to 0 and the character gains an **advancement**.

You can spend advancements to unlock and upgrades your character in different ways, as shown by the table below:

| **Advancement**                        | **Cost** |
| -------------------------------------- | -------- |
| New playbook ability                   | 2        |
| Veteran playbook ability               | 3        |
| Action or attribute rating (see below) | 1 - 4    |

To add an action or attribute dot, you need a number of advancements equal to the new rating.

This means that going from action rating 1 to 2 costs two advancements, or going from 3 to 4 costs four advancements.

When you go from rating 0 to 1 in an action, that costs advancements equal to your attribute rating, not your action rating. So if you had rating in **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Skulk]]** and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Scrap]]** (both **[[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes]]** actions), and wanted to take your first rating in **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Scramble]]** (also a **[[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Prowess\|Prowess]]** action), that would cost 3 advancements, since it is increasing your **[[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Prowess\|Prowess]]** from 2 to 3.

## Spending Advancements

In order to spend your accumulated advancements you must take the [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Train]] downtime activity. While you mark xp when it happens, you must spend time and effort between jobs to better yourself.
