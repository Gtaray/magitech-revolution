# A scrappy and lucky survivor

Neither the most reputable nor the one to choose the safe route, you’re a smooth-talking criminal and luck is just one of your many skills. You might be a charming rogue out for themselves. You might be the convict on the run who always has a trick up their sleeve. But while it’s true that anyone in [[Khett]] can get into trouble, you always get into the very best kind.

**When you play a Scoundrel, you earn xp when you address challenges with charm or audacity.** Ramp up the daring and be quick on the draw with a catchy one-liner. You shine brightest when the situation is just about to get ugly.

_Who was the last person you cheated out of their due? Are you loyal to this crew, or are they just a means to an end? How many times have you been left for dead?_

# Playing a Scoundrel

Scoundrels are one of the most versatile playbooks — your innate luck and daring can give you a chance to shine in many situations. Learn when to push your luck and when to let things ride. Be a risk-taker, but consider the kinds of risks you’ll take and why. Are you always willing to put it on the line for a larger payout? Do you carry a grudge over slights long past?

Strong characterization goes a long way for the Scoundrel. Describe your character’s attitude, their look, and how they walk into a room. Make them big and bold and let everyone have to deal with them. Do you have something to prove? Is your sense of importance earned or bravado? Are you smooth and friendly or brash and rough?

[[3. Position#Desperate|Desperate]] actions earn xp and play into many of your abilities. Don’t shy away from them! Rely on armors ([[Special Armor|special]] and [[Armor|gear]]), [[2. Push Yourself#Resistance Roll|resistance rolls]], and your crew to mitigate the worst of it. A broad ability base helps with resistances and getting away with bad decisions.

For actions, focus on active skills — **[[Rig]]**, **[[Scrap]]**, even **[[Command]]**. **[[Skulk]]** will let you play a familiar pickpocket and card shark. The ship captain stereotype leans on **[[Helm]]**. A good scoundrel is always in the thick of it acting with a hare-brained plan, rather than reacting.

Veteran abilities flesh out a concept. The [[Druid|Druid's]] [[Druid#Shillelagh|Shillelagh]] and most [[Muscle|Muscle’s]] abilities make for tough rogues. Smooth-talking Scoundrels might look at the [[Speaker|Speaker’s]] [[Speaker#Disarming|Disarming]] ability. Rogues with hearts of gold should look at the [[Stitch|Stitch’s]] [[Stitch#Moral Compass|Moral Compass]] or the [[Mechanic|Mechanic’s]] [[Mechanic#Mechanic's Heart|Heart]]. And captains and crew leaders should look at the [[Pilot|Pilot's]] [[Pilot#Commander|Commander]] and the [[Muscle|Muscle's]] [[Muscle#Backup|Backup]] ability.

# Special Abilities

## Starting Ability

### Serendipitous

Your crew starts with **+1 [[5. Gambits|gambit]]** when the pool resets.

_This increases the crew’s starting gambits on a job. You’re just plain luckier than other people._

## Special Abilities

### Shoot First

When you attack from hiding or spring a trap, take **+1d**. When there’s a question about who acts first, the answer is you (two characters with Shoot First act simultaneously).

_To attack from hiding, your target must be unaware of you. If you’re springing a trap, your target must be unaware of the trap._

### Ask Questions Later

When you **[[Consort]]** to gather info, you gain **+1 [[4. Effect|effect]]** and can in addition ask: Who might this benefit?

_When you ask your own question, you learn the answer to that, as well as to the question "who might this benefit?" Since you gain potency, the worst a normal gather information roll can produce would be standard results._

### Daredevil

You can spend your **[[Special Armor]]** to make a **[[3. Position#Desperate|desperate]]** roll **[[3. Position#Risky|risky]]** instead.

_Make this choice before you roll. This affects all other mechanics care about a roll being desperate or risky._

### Devil's Own Luck

You may expend your **[[Special Armor]]** to [[2. Push Yourself#Resistance Roll|resist]] the consequences of gunfire, or to **[[2. Push Yourself|push yourself]]** when talking your way out of (or running from) trouble.

_Gunfire can extend to pistols and being shot in any way, and running from trouble can be via vehicle as much as your feet. If you use this ability to push yourself, you get one of the benefits but you don’t take stress cost._

### I Know a Guy

When you **[[Work]]** a side job, ask the GM about a potential job:

- It’s not deadly
- It pays well enough
- It’s not a rush job
- It comes from a faction you trust
- It targets an enemy you have.

You may spend **1 [[Coin]]** per additional feature.

_Though you keep an ear to the ground, sufficient time has to pass for new jobs to crop up at a port (usually a downtime or two). The GM will tell you how you hear about the work; it might be a publicly available bounty or something a contact reaches out about._

### Never Tell Me the Odds

You generate **[[5. Gambits|gambits]]** on [[3. Position#Desperate|desperate]] rolls, and you may also generate gambits even if you spent a gambit.

_Normally you only generate gambits on risky rolls for which you have not spent a gambit. Your ability to "stretch" gambits, regenerating them on rolls you have already spent them on, gives you the ability to put yourself in situations others might not even want to consider._

### Tenacious

Reduce the **[[Stress]]** cost to **[[Harm#Tough It Out|Tough Out]]** harm by 1.

_Normally Toughing Out harm costs stress equal to the harm level, but for you its one less. This means you can Tough Out level 1 harm for free!_

### When the Chips are Down

You gain a second use of **[[Special Armor]]** between each **[[1. Downtime|downtime]]**.

_This allows you to use a second special armor OR use the same special armor twice._

# Playbook Items

### Enhanced Ammunition

Specially loaded charges that can only be loaded into a firearm designed for this ammo. When taken, decide how the ammunition is enhanced (armor-piercing, silenced, magical, etc). Grants **potency** against such targets. **\[0 Load]**

_Who makes your enhanced bullets? What type of ammo do you prefer or avoid using?_

### [[Loadout#Quality|Fine]] Coat

A heavy but well-made and well-kept coat. Distinctive and with a history. **\[1 Load]**

_Where did you get this coat? Was it a gift, an impulse purchase, or something you won in a bet? Is it decorated with any insignia or logos? Make sure it’s got a memorable look or color._

### Forged Documents

Reasonably well-made facsimiles of documents that would never actually be given to someone like you. **\[0 Load]**

_Who made these for you? Do you still owe someone for them? Or did you steal them from someone?_

### Loaded Dice or Trick Cards

Gambling accoutrements subtly altered to favor particular outcomes. Luck is one of your many skills. Sometimes it just needs a little help though. **\[0 Load]**

_When’s the last time these got you into trouble?_

### Personal Memento

A keepsake you cherish. A locket, small portrait, music from your home. **\[0 Load]**

_For someone so tied to the skies, what about this memento makes it so important? Who does it remind you of? Why do you still hold onto it?_

### Reikland Repeater

Repeating six-shot revolver. Customized to carry enhanced ammunition. **\[1 Load]**

_Where did you get this? Who customized it? Did you name it?_
