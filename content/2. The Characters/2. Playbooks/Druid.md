# A wanderer in touch with the land

While your powers make you a force to be reckoned with in [[8. Glossary/Khett]], far more important is your ability to understand and deal with the natural magics and all things tied to it. From handling ancient artifacts to calming beasts in the deep wilderness, a Druid makes life on the steppes substantially safer.

**When you play a Druid, you earn xp when you address challenges with wisdom or natural magics.** This will naturally push you towards the unknown and inexplicable. When things get weird, that’s your moment to find answers.

_How did you come by your understanding of spiritual magic? Does your circle have any special creed or curious practices? How long have you been traveling?_

# Playing a Druid

Many kinds of Druids wander the steppes. Since there are numerous (often conflicting) beliefs on the nature of things, druid circles spring up around strong opinions about how the world works. Think about how you interact with and perceive these powers. Do you connect to ley lines that crisscross the world? Do you commune with [[8. Glossary/Natural Spirits\|nature spirits]]? Do you use ancient runes to conjure your magic?

How did you learn the techniques and secrets of your circle? Are you even part of a circle? Why do you wander, and what is it you hope to achieve in your travels?

Your crew will rely on you to be their interpreter for nature's many [[8. Glossary/Natural Spirits\|spirits]]. When your crew comes across strange disturbances, filter your explanations through your belief in the primal forces at work in the world. Look for experiences that challenge your beliefs of how these magics work, and grapple with your new understandings. These obstacles bring life to the unusual.

What is your relationship to magitech? Most druids despise it and see it, at best, as a perversion of the natural order of things, and at worst, enslavement of [[8. Glossary/Natural Spirits\|nature spirits]]. How do you balance your feelings with the fact that you travel on an [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]], and that your crew uses magitech?

For actions, if you intend to be a shamanic warrior, you’ll want to pick up **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Scrap]]**, or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Sway]]** and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Consort]]** for those looking to go the more mental or social route. If you want to break the mold and work with magitech, consider **Rig** for constructive work, or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Hack]]** for destructive work.

Good Veteran abilities to consider might be the [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Pilot]]’s [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Pilot#Traveler\|Traveler]] ability for wandering Druids, or the [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Muscle]] abilities for Shillelagh-wielding druid warriors.

# Special Abilities

## Starting Ability

### Guidance

You can spend a **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/5. Gambits\|gambit]]** instead of paying any **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress\|stress]]** cost.

_Any time you need to spend stress, you may spend a gambit instead. This includes costs for Pushing Yourself. For abilities that have a variable cost, such as The Old Tongue, those additional costs are all part of the same activation, so the push and all additional features together only costs one gambit._

## Special Abilities

### Animal Companion

Gain an animal companion cohort, and select its expertise (fighting, scouting, tracking, etc.). This companion obeys your commands and anticipates your actions.

The animal companion has **quality** equal to your [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]] +1. Their [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/4. Effect#Scale\|scale]] is always zero (1 person).

_Your animal companion is not tamed. It is a wild beast with whom you have a mutually respectful friendship. Does it accept your crewmates or barely tolerate them?_

### Call on the Ancients

You know secret ways to commune with ancient powers. When you **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work]]** on a **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work#Long-Term Project\|long-term project]]** to contact such powers, you fill **+1 tick**. Gain **+1d** to **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/2. Push Yourself\|resist]]** the consequences of **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Consort\|Consorting]]** with or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Command\|Commanding]]** these powers.

_Channeling the power of ancient spirits is normally risky proposition at best, as you may not be able to fully control these spirits. But you are especially gifted at avoiding such blowback. Why?_

### Consult the Runes

You can spend **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to increase the effect level of a **[[5. Downtime/4. Downtime Activities\|downtime activity]]** performed by yourself or a crewmate.

_What oracular methods do you prefer? When were they last wrong? What kind of magical rituals might you know?_

### Nature's Ward

You can spend **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to reduce the severity level of a **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm]]** suffered by you or a crewmate you **[[4. The Job/4. Teamwork\|assist]]**.

_This ability is used as a reaction to suffering harm, and can't change harm a character has already suffered._

### Shillelagh

Imbue a non-metal blunt weapon with nature's power, allowing it to strike as though it were magical. Gain **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/4. Effect#Potency\|potency]]** on your melee attacks.

_Imbued weapons are magical, and are effective against things that would normally resist nonmagical damage._

### Spirit Mind

You're always aware of supernatural and spiritual entities in your presence. Take **+1d** whenever you **[[1. How to Play/8. Gathering Information\|gather information]]** about the supernatural and spiritual by any means.

_You are aware of the subtle ways that greater powers influence the world._

### The Old Tongue

You know forgotten words in ancient druidic languages that command obeisance. You may **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/2. Push Yourself\|push yourself]]** to compel a creature (humanoid or bestial) and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Sway]]** them in the face of contradictory or conflicting evidence.

Gain **+1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress\|stress]]** for each additional feature:

- They have only vague memories of the event
- It works on a small group

_Such confusions do not persist indefinitely, though those with vague memories will often fill in the missing details with assumptions. "I must’ve checked her license. I always do."_

### Wild Shape

When you take animal form, you can **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/2. Push Yourself\|Push Yourself]]** to do one of the following:

- Attack with dangerous force
- Move at exceptional speeds, including swimming or flying.
- Solve a problem only an animal could solve

_Most druids can take the form of animals, though far fewer make it their primary strength. Dangerous force means fast enough to severely injure a normal person. Exceptional speed means fast enough to outpace all but the fastest vehicles._

# Druid Items

### Magical Reagents

Incense, dried roots, powders, tinctures, and other magical miscellany that your magics sometimes require. Gain **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/4. Effect#Potency\|potency]]** when performing magic. **\[1 Load\]**

### [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Loadout#Quality\|Masterwork]] Melee Weapon

A simple weapon (often a cudgel or walking stick) that has been masterfully worked. In the age of high magic and [[8. Glossary/Firearms\|firearms]], this might seem antiquated, but it is a humble, honest weapon. **\[2 Load\]**

_Who made this weapon? What training have you had with it? Does it hold sentimental value?_

### Memento of your Travels

A small statue, outdated currency, a lock of hair, a portrait. **\[0 Load\]**

_Who gave this to you, and why do you cherish it? Is it a reminder of a good or bad memory?_

### Mystical Artifact

A small magical object. Not magitech, but an old artifact with esoteric powers.

Work with the GM to determine what this object is, and its powers, cost, and possible side effects. **\[1 Load\]**

_Was this given to you by the circle that trained you? Did you find it as a child? Do you know what it does or are you still unlocking its mysteries?_

### Offerings

A candle, flowers, food, incense, precious stones. **\[0 Load\]**

_Were they gifts, left at a shrine for any druid to take? Or were they given in return for a job?_

### Religious Outfit

Robes, worn cloaks, sandals, etc. Identifies you as a practitioner of old magics, and is unassuming enough that most won't look too deeploy. Baggy enough to sneak 1 Load under light scrutiny. **\[1 Load\]**

_What insignia do you carry for your circle? Is your outfit hand-crafted? Passed down?_
