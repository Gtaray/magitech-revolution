# A healer or scientist

Respected across the region, the skills to heal is one of the most valuable out on the marches. Properly trained physicians are, if not welcomed, at least treated well. For crews that tend to take a few injuries, a Stitch capable of knitting bones and patching bullet wounds is always a welcome addition.

**When you play a Stitch, you earn xp when you address challenges with insight or compassion.** Make sure you see the whole picture and help keep your crew safe and sound. When things get out of hand, they’ll need you. When they lose their way, guide them. Remember that not all wounds are physical.

_What research interests you? What do you charge for your services? How did you learn your trade?_

# Playing a Stitch

When making a Stitch, think about what drives you to tend to this particular crew. Do you care deeply for this crew of scoundrels, or is this a way to keep one step ahead of the law? Equally important is what kind of person you are — are your patients always safe in your care? Would you refuse to help someone you think is a bad person?

Stitches can have varied backgrounds. Some are former trained field medics who may have fallen on hard times. Others are folks that learned through necessity. Some might be scholars whose line of research is not condoned by the guilds.

Your starting ability deserves some attention. With it, you can do two things at the same time. You can substitute your **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor]]** rating for any other action, but more importantly, you get to have a cool moment where you talk about a former patient, posting, or research project that lends you the knowledge you need.

For actions, consider **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Sway]]** and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Consort]]**, if you’re a friendly sort or still have connections in high society. If you used to be a combat medic, **[[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Prowess\|Prowess]]** actions are good.

For Veteran abilities, look at the [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Speaker\|Speaker’s]] [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Speaker#Heart to Heart\|Heart to Heart]] if you’re a caretaker, while the [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Muscle\|Muscle’s]] [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Muscle#Bodyguard\|Bodyguard]] ability allows you to keep the people in your care alive even in the worst of conditions.

Although Stitches are usually on board to tend to wounds, some are pure scientists. Consider starting with [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Stitch#Magic Hands\|Magic Hands]] or [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Stitch#Book Learning\|Book Learning]] in that case. Many [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Mechanic]] abilities might also fit. Also **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Rig]]** and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Hack]]** might be useful to these types.

# Special Abilities

## Starting Ability

### I'm A Doctor, Not A...

You can gain **1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]** to roll your **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor]]** rating while performing a different action. Say which patient, research, or posting taught you this trick.

_Each time you use this ability we learn a little bit more about your past. Consider introducing your contacts in these discoveries, if appropriate._

## Special Abilities

### Book Learning

You speak a multitude of languages and are broadly educated. When **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work\|Working]]** on a **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work#Long-Term Project\|long-term project]]**, or **[[4. The Job/4. Teamwork\|assisting]]** a crewmate with such a project, fill **+1 tick**.

_Communication is almost never an issue for you. Additionally, you have a background in most academic subjects that might come up, and can use gather information checks to find out what you might have learned in those studies._

### Combat Medic

You may expend your **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to allow a crewmate to **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm#Tough It Out\|Tough Out]]** a harm.

_This can be done as a reaction or in the middle of resolving a threat roll, as long as it fits the fiction._

### Magic Hands

Your research and fields of study are fringe, esoteric, and focus on the magical. You may always handle magical artifacts safely. When you **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Study]]** an artifact or **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor]]** a strange substance, you may ask one: _what could this do?_ _why could this be dangerous?_

_Normally it takes an Attune roll to handle a magical artifact safely. When you ask your questions, this may be in addition to whatever else you were doing with the artifact or substance. Treat this like a gather information roll where you rolled a 6._

### Moral Compass

When you do the right thing at a cost to yourself, you and one crewmate each recover **1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]**, or **2 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Stress]]** if the cost was major.

_The cost to yourself must be real, though it need not be devastating. Losing a meaningful opportunity, experiencing a setback with a project, or getting into a heated argument with a friend could all count._

### Patch

You can expend your **[[1. How to Play/5. Armor & Special Armor/Special Armor]]** to **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/2. Push Yourself\|Push Yourself]]** when **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor\|Doctoring]]**. Your crew's **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Recover\|Recoveries]]** fill **+1 tick**.

_Your special armor can be applied to any use of Doctor, either for resisting the consequences or to increase an action's effect._

### Physicker

Once per **[[5. Downtime/1. Downtime\|downtime]]** when you tend to a crewmate that **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Recover\|Recovers]]**, you can remove a [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]] of level equal to or less than your **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Doctor]]** rating. Spend **1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** for each additional use of this.

_This effect normally requires a medical professional or hospital of quality 3 or higher. This lets you perform that service at a hefty discount._

### Under Pressure

Add a **[[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/5. Gambits\|gambit]]** to the pool whenever you or a crew member suffers level 2 or greater [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]].

_In order to gain the gambit, the resulting applied consequence must be level 2 or greater—if it is resisted or reduced below that level, you do not gain the gambit._

### Welcome Anywhere

While wearing your medic garb, you are welcome even in dangerous places. Gain **+1d** to **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Consort]]** and **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Sway]]** when offering to tend to anyone in need.

_When you choose to be, you’re recognizable as a medic, and of value to those living the hard life of Khett._
