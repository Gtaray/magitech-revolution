There are three attributes in the game system that the player characters use to resist bad consequences: **Insight**, **Prowess**, and **Resolve**. Each attribute has a rating (from zero to four) that tells you how many dice to roll when using that attribute.

The rating for each attribute is equal to the number of actions under that attribute in which you have at least one point. The more well-rounded your character is with a particular set of actions, the better their attribute rating.

### Insight

Insight represents your character's intellect, intuition, and critical thinking skills. Insight actions are: [[Doctor]], [[Hack]], [[Rig]], and [[Study]]

### Prowess

Prowess is your character's physical ability; strength, fortitude, agility, and reaction time. Prowess actions are: [[Helm]], [[Scramble]], [[Scrap]], and [[Skulk]]

### Resolve

Resolve represents your character's willpower, charisma, and influence. Resolve actions are: [[Attune]], [[Command]], [[Consort]], and [[Sway]]

## Attribute Rolls

To make an attribute roll, roll **1d** for each rating in the **attribute** you're using, plus any **bonus dice** you have.
