Some special abilities refer to your **special armor**. If you have any abilities that uses your special armor, tick its box when you activate one of them. If you don't have any special abilities that use special armor, then you can't use that armor box at all.

Like normal [[Armor]], special armor is restored when you select your **[[Loadout|load]]** before a [[1. The Job|job]].
