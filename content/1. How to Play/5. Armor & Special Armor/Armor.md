If you have a type of **armor** that applies to the situation, you can mark an armor box to reduce or avoid a consequence, instead of rolling to resist.

## Heavy Armor
When you have **Heavy Armor**, you have a second use of armor and can mark a second armor box.

## Restoring Armor
When an armor box is marked, it can’t be used again until it’s restored. All of your armor is restored when you choose your **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Loadout\|load]]** before a [[4. The Job/1. The Job\|job]].
