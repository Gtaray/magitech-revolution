Occasionally something requires your complete and undivided attention, to the point that it would be difficult to think or do anything else. Most commonly concentration is required to maintain powerful persistent spells or magical effects.

This is represented by a level 1 **[[Harm]]** called "Concentration." When you start concentrating on something, you gain this level 1 harm. When you stop concentrating on something, you remove that harm. You cannot gain the "Concentration" harm multiple times, and thus, cannot concentrate on multiple things simultaneously.

Concentration can be invoked like any other harm to affect the fictional position of your character (and gain you an XP when you do so). Having reduced effect on anything you're not concentrating on is a simple and common way to invoke this harm. Unlike other harms, you cannot **[[Harm#Tough It Out|tough out]]** concentration. The only way to avoid the effects of concentration is to stop concentrating.
