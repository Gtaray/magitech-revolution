Player characters have a special reserve of fortitude and luck called **stress**. When they suffer a consequence that they don’t want to accept, they can take stress instead. The result a **[[2. Push Yourself#Resistance Roll|resistance]] roll** determines how much stress it costs to avoid a bad outcome.

> _During a shootout, Alice’s character, Hayley, gets shot in the chest. Alice rolls her Prowess rating to push herself to resist, and gets a 2, meaning it costs 3 stress to resist the consequences. Alice marks off 3 stress and describes how Hayley survives._
>
> _The GM rules that the harm is reduced by the resistance roll, but not avoided entirely. Hayley suffers level 2 harm “Chest Wound” instead of level 3 harm “Punctured Lung._
