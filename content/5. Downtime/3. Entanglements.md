Your characters and crew didn’t just spring into existence tonight. You have a complex history of favors, commitments, debts, and promises that got you where you are today. To reflect this, after each job, you roll dice to find out which entanglement comes calling. An entanglement might be a rival crew looking to throw their weight around (and demand some Coin), a clan enforcer making a case against your crew (but ready for a bribe), or even the attention of a strange creature.

After **[[4. The Job/5. Payoff\|payoff]]** and **[[4. The Job/6. Heat\|heat]]** are determined, the GM generates an entanglement for the crew using the lists below. Find the column that matches the crew’s current **[[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted Level]]**. Then roll a die to select which sort of entanglement manifests.

Entanglements manifest fully before the PCs have a chance to avoid them. When an entanglement comes into play, describe the situation after the entanglement has manifested. The PCs deal with it from that point—they can’t intercept it and defuse it before it happens. The purpose of the mechanic is to abstract the complex stuff happening in the world and the background of the crew’s lives. As the GM, keep an eye out to bring in friends, rivals, old enemies, and the plots of the sector in general via entanglements.

Entanglements are the cost of doing business in the underworld—a good crew learns to roll with the punches and pick their battles.

The entanglements are detailed on the following paragraphs. Each has a list of potential ways for the PCs to resolve them. If you want the entanglement to be a momentary problem for the crew, stick to the suggested methods to resolve it, and move on to the next part of downtime. If you want to dive in and explore the entanglement in detail, set the scene and play out the event in full, following the actions and consequences where they lead.

#### Arrest
A detective presents a case file of evidence to a judge, to begin prosecution of your crew. The clan authorities send a group to arrest you (a gang at least equal in scale to your **[[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted Level]]** or a ship of that [[6. Factions/2. Faction Tier\|tier]]). Pay them off with [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]] equal to your **Wanted Level** +3, hand someone over for arrest (this clears your **[[4. The Job/6. Heat\|Heat]]** and **Wanted Level** in the [[8. Glossary/Khorvate\|region]]), or try to evade capture.

#### Bounty Hunter
An enemy faction hires a bounty hunter. Fight, evade, or pay them off. If you have no [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] with negative status, you avoid entanglements right now.

Bounty hunters are strong fighters, with gear that grant them **potency** in combat. Base their [[6. Factions/2. Faction Tier\|tier]] on the hiring faction and how angry they are. Pay them off with **3 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]**.

#### Collectors
A collection agency claims part of your ship. Pay them **2 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]**, give them the components (remove a ship module), or make them leave another way.

#### Cooperation
A [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] you have +2 or +3 **[[6. Factions/3. Faction Status\|status]]** with asks you for a “favor.” Agree to it or lose a status with them. If you don’t have a +2 or +3 [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] **[[6. Factions/3. Faction Status\|status]]**, you avoid entanglements right now.

#### Impounded
Skydock authorities impound your craft. Bribe dockworkers to lift the lockdown with 2 **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]**, or steal your ship back. If you are not in a place with a skydock, you avoid entanglements right now.

#### Interrogation
Clan authorities round up one of the PCs to question them about the crew’s crimes. How did they manage to capture you? Either pay them off with **2 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]**, or they sweat it out of you (level 2 **[[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]]**) AND you tell them what they want to know (**+2 [[4. The Job/6. Heat\|Heat]]**). You can resist each of those consequences separately.

#### New "Friends"
A neutral [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] asks you to do a job that is either extra dangerous, pays little, or gathers more [[4. The Job/6. Heat\|Heat]] than usual (GM’s choice). Accept or lose a **[[6. Factions/3. Faction Status\|status]]** with them

#### Out of Fuel
A piece of your ship fails catastrophically, [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems#System Damage\|damaging the system]] and rendering it inoperable until [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Repair\|repaired]]. If you didn’t use your ship at all on the last job, you avoid entanglements right now.

#### Brigands!
Brigands attempt to take over your ship by force. Outrun, fight, or cut a deal. If you didn’t use your ship at all on the last job, you avoid entanglements right now.

#### Reprisals
An enemy [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] moves against you or yours. Pay them **1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** per [[6. Factions/2. Faction Tier\|tier]], allow them to mess with you, or fight back. If you have no [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]] with negative **[[6. Factions/3. Faction Status\|status]]**, you avoid entanglements right now.

#### Ship Trouble
A [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems\|ship system]] acts up. [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems#System Damage\|Damage]] a system (the GM will tell you which).

#### Unquiet Skies
A creature finds its way on board. Acquire the services of an exterminator to destroy or banish it, or deal with it yourself.

#### The Spirits Speak
Strange forces calls out to each of you in a language you cannot understand. Resist with **[[1. How to Play/2. Actions & Attributes/Attributes#Resolve\|Resolve]]** or take level 1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm]] from the visions you cannot escape.

#### Wanted Level 4: Warship
The government sends a warship to capture your airship. When you hit **[[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted Level]] 4** in a system, the thane's personal guard is sent out to bring you to justice, and you will have to find a way to get away. You cannot bribe them, and fighting them would be foolhardy. In any case, if you survive the encounter, your [[4. The Job/6. Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted Level]] drops by 1 — resources for your pursuit aren’t infinite.

Generally the warship will be a well-armed frigate with a few personal flight craft. You may want to consider repainting or modifying your ship, or getting fake papers to help shake similar experiences off your tail.
