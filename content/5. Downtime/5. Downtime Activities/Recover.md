When you recover in a safe location, you gain **ticks** on your **healing clock** equal to your **[[5. Crew Quality|Crew Quality]]+1**. When your healing clock fills, reduce each instance of [[Harm|harm]] you have by one level. When you remove all [[Harm|harm]], clear your healing clock.

If you also engage the services of a physicker, they remove an instance of harm with a level equal to or less than their **Quality**. For each **[[Coin|coin]]** you pay, increase the Physicker’s Quality by one for this downtime, to receive more extensive care. A [[Stitch]] with the [[Stitch#Physicker|Physicker]] ability can act in this way, and their Quality is equal to their [[Doctor]] [[Actions|rating]].

> _When you recover, how are you vulnerable? Do you show the pain of your injuries, open up to someone emotionally, or something else?_

# Hospital

Instead of recovering on your own, use this activity to stay in a private **hospital**. Remove all of your harm and choose: Take **1 [[6. Heat|Heat]]** and pay **2 [[Coin|coin]]** -or- pay **3 [[Coin|coin]]**.
