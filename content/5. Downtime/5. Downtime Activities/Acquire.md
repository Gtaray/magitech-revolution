Gain temporary items, services, vehicles, or specialists for the crew. Make a **[[3. Progress Clocks|4-segment supply |clock]]** for the acquisition, to be ticked each time it’s used in a consequential way.

- Pay **1 [[Coin|coin]]** for every level of **Quality** higher than the crew’s Tier.
- Pay **+1 [[Coin|coin]]** for a special feature (hidden compartments, disguised or easily concealable, waterproof, etc.).
- If the acquisition is very rare, heavily regulated, restricted, or prohibited, ask the GM how much extra **coin** and/or **[[6. Heat|heat]]** is required to get it, or if you’ll need to acquire it with a **[[1. The Job|job]]**.

Generally, the whole crew gains access to what’s acquired, counting each individual member’s usage against the supply clock.

> _The crew acquires the services of a veteran physicker (Quality 3) to treat them during [[Recover|recovery]]. Since they’re [[5. Crew Quality|crew quality]] 0, this costs 3 coin. Each time the physicker treats a crew member, they tick the usage clock. After four treatments, the physicker’s service ends._

You may acquire a personal item in the same manner, comparing its **[[Loadout#Quality|Quality]]** to your **[[Stash#Lifestyle Rating|lifestyle rating]]**.

> _Where do you go and who do you deal with to acquire what you seek? You can ask the GM if they’ll accept a favor instead of coin._
