When you repair, you take the time to buy the appropriate parts and oversee major fixes to the ship. If you want to repair a crossed wire or a sparking oscillator, you just need a **[[2. The Characters/3. Actions/Rig]]** roll. These kinds of repairs fix battle damage and bring back systems from the brink.

When you take the repair action, **spend 1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]] and clear one damage on a [[3. The Crew/3. Airships/Ship Systems\|ship system]]**. The [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Mechanic\|Mechanic’s]] [[2. The Characters/2. Playbooks/Mechanic#Bailing Wire and Twine\|Bailing Wire and Twine]] ability allows you to do some of the work yourself, and use junk found on the cheap to fix up your ship, removing the need for the **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** to be spent and making repairs faster and cheaper.
