When you repair, you take the time to buy the appropriate parts and oversee major fixes to the ship. If you want to repair a crossed wire or a sparking oscillator, you just need a **[[Rig]]** roll. These kinds of repairs fix battle damage and bring back systems from the brink.

When you take the repair action, **spend 1 [[Coin]] and clear one damage on a [[Ship Systems|ship system]]**. The [[Mechanic|Mechanic’s]] [[Mechanic#Bailing Wire and Twine|Bailing Wire and Twine]] ability allows you to do some of the work yourself, and use junk found on the cheap to fix up your ship, removing the need for the **[[Coin]]** to be spent and making repairs faster and cheaper.
