Between jobs, your crew spends time at their liberty, attending to personal needs and side projects. These are called **downtime activities**. During a **[[5. Downtime/1. Downtime\|downtime]]** phase, each PC has time for **two downtime activities**. When you’re at [[6. Factions/3. Faction Status#Faction Status Levels\|War]] with a [[6. Factions/1. All Factions\|faction]], each PC has time for only one.

You may choose to do the same activity more than once, and the crew may do these in any order. You can only attempt actions that you’re in a position to accomplish. If an activity hinges on another action, resolve that action first.

These are the downtime activities:
- [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Acquire]]
- [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Recover]]
- [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Repair]]
- [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Train]]
- [[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Work]]

A PC can make time for more than two activities, at a cost. **Each additional activity costs 1 [[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]**. This reflects the time and resource drain while you’re “off the clock” and not earning from a job. When you complete a new job, you reset and get two “free” activities again.

Activities on the downtime list are limited; normal actions are not. During downtime, you can still go places, do things, make [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/1. Threat Roll\|threat rolls]], [[1. How to Play/8. Gathering Information\|gather information]], talk with other characters, etc. In other words, only activities that are on the list are limited.

For any downtime activity, you may spend **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** after the roll to improve the result level. Increase the result level by one for each **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** spent. So, a **1-3** result becomes a **4/5**, a **4/5** result becomes a **6**, and a **6** becomes a **critical**. If the activity requires a roll and you can involve a contact on your sheet or the ship sheet, take **+1d**.

Frequently players will want to decide on what job they’re about to do next, so they can prepare for it by **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Acquire\|acquiring assets]]**, and **[[1. How to Play/8. Gathering Information]]**. Didn’t think of the right thing to bring on a job? You can **[[4. The Job/3. Flashbacks\|flash back]]** to a **downtime activity** by paying a **[[2. The Characters/4. Rules/Coin]]** as normal.

GM: If a player can’t decide which downtime activity to pick, offer them a long-term project idea. You know what the player is interested in and what they like. Suggest a project that will head in a fun direction for them. Suggest they look into what their rivals or factions they’re interested in are up to.

> *“Remember that strange song you heard when you opened the relic engine before you passed out? Yeah, do you want to get to the bottom of that? Okay, start a long-term project — six segments — called ‘Song in the Engine.’ What action do you use to work on that?”*

## Heal Level 1 Harm
When starting downtime activities, the crew removes all of their **level 1 [[1. How to Play/4. Harm, Stress & Trauma/Harm\|harm]]**. If you have a safe place to rest, add **one tick** to your **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Recover\|healing clock]]**.

## Indulging Vice
At any point during **downtime activities**, a character may **[[5. Downtime/5. Downtime Activities/Indulge Vice\|indulge their vice]]**. This does not count as a downtime activity.
