Accomplish a **project** or work a **side job** to earn **1 [[2. The Characters/Rules/Coin\|coin]]**. Ask the GM if you can charge more (especially if you work for a wealthy client) or gain some other benefit instead of coin (like earning a favor, reducing **[[4. The Job/Heat]]**, etc.).

Note: this game is about rogues doing crime, not having regular jobs, so consider limiting side jobs to one per PC during [[5. Downtime/Downtime\|downtime]]. 

> *Who do you do work for? Ask the GM if there’s anything going on with them. What challenge do you address on your project?*

# Long-Term Project
For a **long-term project**, make a **[[1. How to Play/The Basics/Progress Clocks\|progress clock]]**. When you spend an activity working on it, mark a number of ticks equal to your **[[1. How to Play/Actions & Attributes/Actions\|action rating]]**. Add **one tick** if you have a **[[3. The Crew/Ship Upgrades/Auxiliary Upgrades#Workshop\|workshop]]** (or some other type of appropriate advantage). Elaborate projects require multiple clocks (and may also call for a successful score to fully complete).

To create something that doesn't require a progress clock, consider **[[5. Downtime/Downtime Activities/Craft\|crafting]]** it.

# Lowering Wanted Level
Since **[[4. The Job/Heat]]** reduction is handled immediately following a score, the former "Lay Low" downtime activity has been rolled into this downtime activity. The standard way of lowering your **[[4. The Job/Heat#Wanted Level\|Wanted Level]]** in a region is by doing [[4. The Job/Heat#Wanted Level\|jail time]]. But that's not ideal, so an alternative is to **Work** on a scheme to clear your **Wanted Level**.

These schemes can be a combination of **Long-Term Projects**, **Side Jobs**, and potentially culminate in a **[[4. The Job/The Job\|job]]** to pull it all together.

> *The crew has risen to Wanted Level 3, and all the while, Scarlet has been working on long-term projects to frame their most hated enemy, Master Slane, and send him away for good. During downtime, she Consorts to plant rumors and finish the final clock in the project. The crew can now pull off a score to break into Slane’s office and plant the most damning evidence right before they tip off the Inspector who’s been investigating the case.*
