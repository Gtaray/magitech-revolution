A band of ferocious brigands and pirates hiding among the remote craggy cliffs in the unincorporated highlands of [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Durvos]].

**Tier:** 3
**Turf:** The Cave in [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Durvos]] (HQ). Durven highlands.
**NPCs:**
- Suvi "The Desert Rose", Bandit Queen. Beautiful, mirthful, risk-taker
- Asko "Thorn", Captain. Quiet, calculating, deadly.
- Donnel "Skiff", Captain. Ex-Noble, daring, sky-savvy.

**Notable Assets:** Hullbreaker (wyvern-class [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]]). Several dragonfly-class airships. Former (or kidnapped?) Guild engineers and airship mechanics.
**Quirks:** The Maelstrom's hideout ("The Cave") is cobbled together from the scuttled wrecks of airships, and is hidden by a modified [[3. The Crew/Ship Upgrades/Support Upgrades#Sky Cloak\|Sky Cloak]] module.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Criminal/Ashen Knives]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Earthquake]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]], The Guilds
**Situation:** The Maelstrom raids cargo lanes (including the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]]) and sells goods via the [[6. Factions/Criminal/Ashen Knives]]. Rose is scheming up a plan to heist a dragon-class airship and use it to seize control of a shipyard.
**Goals:**
- Steal a dragon-class military airship
- Seize control of a shipyard
