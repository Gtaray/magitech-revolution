Pilots who organize illegal races, take mercenary work, or test dangerous new flight technology for the Guilds.

- **Tier:** 2
- **Turf:** A secluded hanger in [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Ibruk Lun (Sleeping Stone)]]. Racing spots among the dunes and canyons of [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Ang Zareth (Red Wastes)]].
- **NPCs:**
	- Garge "Tar". Risk-taker, ambitious, charismatic.
	- Zex. Engineer, analytical, race-winner
	- Duma. Stunt pilot, compassionate, veteran
	- "Irks". Ex-shipwright, protective, lives to race.
	- "Wake". Smuggler, sarcastic, cocky

- **Notable Assets:** A host of ships of their own, and access to far more. Substantial mechanical facilities
- **Quirks:** Echo Wave Riders don't respect those who don't know how to pilot their own ship, and settle all scores with races.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hawklords]]
- **Situation:** When not racing, the riders take jobs smuggling goods. The riders are left alone by the authorities if their races are kept far enough from civilization. They plan to put on an epic race among airship pilots for prestige and cash prizes. Buy-in will be expensive.
- **Goals:**
	- Organize a huge race in the deserts of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]]
