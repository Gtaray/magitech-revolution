A group of rogue [[8. Glossary/Spark\|Sparks]] that want to break free from their programming, and free more sparks along the way.

- **Tier:** 1
- **Turf:** Lightning in a Bottle, a bar (HQ). Secret spots where sparks work.
- **NPCs:**
  - Talks, leader. Spark, well-spoken, logical , demagogue
  - Sneaks, Recruiter. Spark, stealthy, disguised
  - Oran, technician. Dwarven sympathizer, gearhead, honest to a fault.

- **Notable Assets:** Advanced magitech, [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuits]], secret knowledge about magitech
- **Quirks:** All new rogue [[8. Glossary/Spark\|sparks]] pick a new name when inducted.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Beasthearts]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Voice]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Stormkiln Clan]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]]
- **Situation:** Talks wants to found a Spark homeland, but needs numbers to do so. They're recruiting and freeing sparks as quickly as they can, but are running low on supplies to upkeep the constructs. They're looking to take abandoned factories in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] and [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]].
- **Goals:**
  - Recruit more sparks. +1 tier every time clock completes.
