Mostly young, disenfranchised dwarves who have turned to crime and found strength and solidarity with each other.

- **Tier:** 1
- **Turf:** Lost Cause, a club with an attached warehouse where the gang does its business (HQ).
- **NPCs:**
  - Dyrinek, Leader. Revolutionary, proud.
  - Burn, Druid. Fast, overconfident, untrained.
  - Radds, Hacker. Smart, punk
  - Myrk, Thug. Gun enthusiast, hothead.

- **Notable Assets:** A few minor artifacts and relics some of the leaders have attached to themselves.
- **Quirks:** When a new member joins the gang, they go all out on a drunken tear across the city, leaving a swath of wreckage in their wake.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthquake]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Broadsheet Guild]]
- **Situation:** The gang has had its run-ins with the authorities and the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Broadsheet Guild]] paints them as lowlifes with no future. Dyrinek wants to send a message that they won't be so easily dismissed, and plans to take over the airwaves to broadcast the gang's punk ideology. All they need is the opportunity.
- **Goals:**
  - Take over a [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Sender's Guild]] relay station.
