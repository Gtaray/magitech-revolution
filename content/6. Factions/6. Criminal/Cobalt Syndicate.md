Once a labor union, the Cobalt Syndicate has turned to smuggling and extortion to carve out some turf and have a real say. Highly decentralized, so can be found nearly everywhere, but because of that their influence is pretty limited.

- **Tier:** 1
- **Turf:** The Pit, a mining quarry (HQ). A major berth and associated hangars.
- **NPCs:**
  - Jek, Leader. Cold, killer, arrogant
  - Kress, Captain. Augmented, defiant, enterprising
  - Usko, Jek's sibling. Thug, daring, envious, gambler.

- **Notable Assets:** A few small [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]] used for smuggling. Laborers in almost every city who wear the Cobalt stripe.
- **Quirks:** Every member wears a solid blue stripe somewhere on their work clothes. A blue strip on the walls of a hangar or warehouse is a call to action.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Voice]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Sender's Guild]]
- **Situation:** Under Jek's command, goods going on the [[8. Glossary/Lightning Rail]] have been getting "taxed", and information on the shipments are passed to [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]] in case they want to rob the train. The two have a good working relationship. In anticipation of a full on civil war, the the Blues have been stockpiling weapons and equipment for what seems like an inevitable and bloody fight.
- **Goals:**
  - Unify the labor force
