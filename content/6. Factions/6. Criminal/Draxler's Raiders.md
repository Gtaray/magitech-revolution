Violent pirates who disable ships before boarding, ransoming crew and cargo alike.

- **Tier:** 3

- **Turf:** Abandoned surface mining outpost (HQ)

- **NPCs:**
  - Draxler, Leader. Killer, vengeful, wild.
  - Skyhawk, Captain. Loyal, vicious, wary.
  - Wolf, Captain. Enforcer, cold, physical.

- **Notable Assets:** Mining [[Airship|airships]] retrofitted with grapplers. Illegal airship modules, like the [[3. Weapon Upgrades#Thaumogravic Pulse Cannon|Thaumogravic Pulse Cannon]], [[4. Support Upgrades#Sky Cloak|Sky Cloak]], and [[4. Support Upgrades#Weave Disrupter|Weave Disrupter]]. Prisoners for ransom. Caches of goods stolen from raided ships.

- **Quirks:** Almost all members of Draxler's Raiders are wanted for crimes.

- **Allies:** [[The Iron Sights]], [[Heartfire Clan]]

- **Enemies:** [[21st Squadron]], [[Deeprock]], [[The Maelstrom]]

- **Situation:** Draxler has made a lot of waves since taking over the Raiders. The crew has decidedly anarchist views. A few bad fights with the [[21st Squadron]] has landed a number of the Raiders' captains in [[Deeprock Hold]], and they have set their eyes on eliminating the prison from [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]] altogether, killing two birds with one stone.

- **Goals:**
  - Pull of a jailbreak at [[Deeprock Hold]]
