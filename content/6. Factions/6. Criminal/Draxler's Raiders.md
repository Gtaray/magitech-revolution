Violent pirates who disable ships before boarding, ransoming crew and cargo alike.

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** Abandoned surface mining outpost (HQ)
- **NPCs:**
  - Draxler, Leader. Killer, vengeful, wild.
  - Skyhawk, Captain. Loyal, vicious, wary.
  - Wolf, Captain. Enforcer, cold, physical.

- **Notable Assets:** Mining [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airships]] retrofitted with grapplers. Illegal airship modules, like the [[3. The Crew/4. Ship Upgrades/3. Weapon Upgrades#Thaumogravic Pulse Cannon\|Thaumogravic Pulse Cannon]], [[3. The Crew/4. Ship Upgrades/4. Support Upgrades#Sky Cloak\|Sky Cloak]], and [[3. The Crew/4. Ship Upgrades/4. Support Upgrades#Weave Disrupter\|Weave Disrupter]]. Prisoners for ransom. Caches of goods stolen from raided ships.
- **Quirks:** Almost all members of Draxler's Raiders are wanted for crimes.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Iron Sights]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Heartfire Clan]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Deeprock]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]]
- **Situation:** Draxler has made a lot of waves since taking over the Raiders. The crew has decidedly anarchist views. A few bad fights with the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]] has landed a number of the Raiders' captains in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Deeprock Hold]], and they have set their eyes on eliminating the prison from [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]] altogether, killing two birds with one stone.
- **Goals:**
  - Pull of a jailbreak at [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Deeprock Hold]]
