Although they often hire themselves out as bounty hunters, the Green Dragons are an infamous group of assassins.

- **Tier:** 4
- **Turf:** The Killroom, a high-end safehouse in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Ramthas]]. Hideouts in most cities.
- **NPCs:**
	- "Uru". Leader, dangerous, retired.
	- "Mamba". Sniper, killer, thrill-seeker.
	- "Shark". Aloof, confident, merciless.

- **Notable Assets:** A large armory of the very best weapons. Custom enchanted gear. An awakened drakehound that's been enhanced, kept by Uru. They feed it others who can't pay their debts.
- **Quirks:** Each member of the Green Dragons bears a distinctive tattoo of a serpent holding a star in its mouth. 
- **Allies:** None. With the Dragons it's strictly business.
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Dragonshield Clan]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Deepforge Clan]]
- **Situation:** The Green Dragons are contract killers specializing in hard targets. They have a reputation for delivering, minimizing collateral, and never taking bribes. They've taken a large contract from the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]] to kill a number of the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]]' heads, but need time to set up the hit.
- **Goals:** 
	- Murder top [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers\|guild]] engineers
