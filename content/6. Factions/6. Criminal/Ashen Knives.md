Once lean and battle ready, the Ashen Knives are a decadent Syndicate focused on drugs, gambling, and pleasures of the flesh.

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** Drug dens. Gambling houses. A hidden and reinforced bunker in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] (HQ).
- **NPCs:**
	- Usan Krin. Sly, corpulent, sartorial, decadent.
	- Knife Lirik. Assassin, gambler, deadly, graceful
	- Oya. High ranking, greedy, well-armed, natural leader

- **Notable Assets:** A crew of well-trained assassins, often with magic artifacts up their sleeves. Heavy coffers. Bribed judges. Crews of thugs. Lots of drugs.
- **Quirks:** To join the syndicate, Knives must take a life. Being promoted requires doing unsavory tasks. Their regional leaders are titled "Usan".
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]]
- **Situation:** The Ashen Knives' power in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] often eclipses that of the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Amberspire Clan]]. This hasn't become a problem yet, since the Amberspire's are more focused on magitech production. A recent alliance with [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]] has filled the Knives' coffer, and now their sights are set on expanding. They've got a new drug on the market called _space rock_.
- **Goals:**
	- Expand into black market magitech
