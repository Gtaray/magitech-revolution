A small enclave of secretive druids who read portents in the stars, dedicated to seeking a set of ancient mystical artifacts

**Tier:** 2
**Turf:** [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Torhmek]] and [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Oghrann]]. A small hideout in the hills near [[7. Duradin/Cities/Giant's Fall]] (HQ)
**NPCs:**
- Doraam, Keeper of Sight. Masked, mysterious, driven.
- Ismissa. Robed, unarmed, magically powerful
- Rakeen. Fighter, physical, huge, brutal

**Notable Assets:** Druidic powers, mostly stellar. Physical prowess via wild shape. A collection of magical artifacts.
**Quirks:** Members' eyes glow with starlight at night and wield potent magic. Most where masks. Some say they speak to voices that come from the stars.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Earthen Enclave]]
**Situation:** Though few, each member is a powerful druid. They hire themselves out to powerful individuals while searching for a set of relics called the Raiment of Night. Doraam has become Magnar Kosoth [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan\|Bloodhammer]]'s personal mystic and advisor after sensing a piece of the Raiment - the Aleph Key.
**Goals:**
- Quietly acquire The Aleph Key
