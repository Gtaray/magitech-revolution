Dwarves whose ancestors made a pact with an ancient spirit of the earth to transform themselves into elementals, eschewing all organic biology for near immortality as beings of rock and stone.

**Tier:** 3
**Turf:** [[7. Duradin/Cities/Hrakhammar]], [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Ladur]] (HQ). Auction halls and warehouses in large cities.
**NPCs:**
- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Enok Deepforge. Flawless, calculating, patient
- Durran. Warrior, ancient, huge, fast.
- Bharryl. Mystic, selective, enthralling. 

**Notable Assets:** Centuries of experience. [[8. Glossary/Adamantine]] equipment and the forges to make more.
**Quirks:** Deepforge dwarves are not born, they are sculpted out of a rare stone and given life through secret rituals. No more than one dwarf is created every ten years.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Brightstone Clan]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Spark of Life]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Deeprock]], [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Acolytes of Many]], [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Circle of Stars]]
**Situation:** The Deepforge dwarves are an enigma to most; they live deeper than others dare dwell and are rarely seen on the surface. Their goals are centuries in the making, as they alone can afford such patience. While slow to adopt magitech, there is a growing idea among the dwarves that it is the next stage in their own evolution, and many seek ways to enhance their body with it.
**Goals:**
- Enact the ritual of geogenesis
- Acquire the recipe for [[8. Glossary/Duranium]]
