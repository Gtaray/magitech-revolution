Primal druids that have made special pacts with wild beasts. They travel the lands, resolving issues related to wild animals.

- **Tier:** 2

- **Turf:** The wild and unincorporated parts of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]].

- **NPCs:**
  - Root, leader. Earth elemental companion, stubborn, old fashioned.
  - Pakka, hunter. Drake hound companion, rowdy, violent.
  - Tosko, guide. Wolf companion, wise, pacifist.

- **Notable Assets:** All manner of wild & weird beasty at their command.

- **Quirks:** The beasthearts meet once a year at a secret conclave.

- **Allies:** [[Hawklords]], [[Spark of Life]]

- **Enemies:** [[Sender's Guild]], [[Ashen Knives]], [[Bloodhammer Clan]]

- **Situation:** The connection a beastheart has to their companion is a strange thing; it's not friendship, and it's not domestication. It's more of a mutual agreement. This leads folk to distrust the beasthearts; whose to say which animals aren't their spies when a beastheart is around? These druids have their sights set on claiming a sandwyrm, something that has historically always failed. For what purpose none can say for sure, but rumors are they want to bring the wyrm to bear against the factories of [[Durvos]] and [[Ladur]]

- **Goals:**
  - Tame a [[Sandwyrm|sandwyrm]]
