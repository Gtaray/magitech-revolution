Primal druids that have made special pacts with wild beasts. They travel the lands, resolving issues related to wild animals.

- **Tier:** 2
- **Turf:** The wild and unincorporated parts of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]].
- **NPCs:**
  - Root, leader. Earth elemental companion, stubborn, old fashioned.
  - Pakka, hunter. Drake hound companion, rowdy, violent.
  - Tosko, guide. Wolf companion, wise, pacifist.

- **Notable Assets:** All manner of wild & weird beasty at their command.
- **Quirks:** The beasthearts meet once a year at a secret conclave.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hawklords]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Spark of Life]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Sender's Guild]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]]
- **Situation:** The connection a beastheart has to their companion is a strange thing; it's not friendship, and it's not domestication. It's more of a mutual agreement. This leads folk to distrust the beasthearts; whose to say which animals aren't their spies when a beastheart is around? These druids have their sights set on claiming a sandwyrm, something that has historically always failed. For what purpose none can say for sure, but rumors are they want to bring the wyrm to bear against the factories of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] and [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]]
- **Goals:**
  - Tame a [[8. Glossary/Sandwyrm\|sandwyrm]]
