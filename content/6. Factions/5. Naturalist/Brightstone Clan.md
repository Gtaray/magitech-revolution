A smaller [[Clan|clan]] that owns and operates the only mithril mines in all of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]].

- **Tier:** 3

- **Turf:** The largest mithril mine in all of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. A fortified distribution center (HQ). Shops in cities.

- **NPCs:**
  - [[Magnar]] Kanuf Brightstone. Charismatic, shrewd, manipulative
  - Uzol, Foreman of the Dauja Mine. Stubborn, protective, hard-working, addicted
  - Sula, Merchant. Connected, professional, skilled

- **Notable Assets:** Stockpiles of mithril. High quality equipment.

- **Quirks:** Mining mithril requires using dangerous chemicals. The dwarves here purposefully inhale the fumes to connect to their presumed ancestors.

- **Allies:** [[Deepforge Clan]], [[Echo Wave Riders]], [[Circle of Stars]]

- **Enemies:** [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Hailstone Clan]]

- **Situation:** The [[Hailstone Clan]] put restrictions on mithril exports from [[Oghrann]] to other [[Khorvate|khorvates]] and this has left the Brightstone Clan with product they can't easily sell. This has led to mines shutting down entirely and leaving folks without a livelihood. Magnar Kanuf has struck a deal with the [[Echo Wave Riders]], who will buy their mithril and smuggle it black markets.

- **Goals:**
  - Become the [[Khorvar]] of [[Oghrann]]
