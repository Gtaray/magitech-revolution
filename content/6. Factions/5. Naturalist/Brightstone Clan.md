A smaller [[8. Glossary/Clan\|clan]] that owns and operates the only mithril mines in all of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]].

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** The largest mithril mine in all of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. A fortified distribution center (HQ). Shops in cities.
- **NPCs:**
  - [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Kanuf Brightstone. Charismatic, shrewd, manipulative
  - Uzol, Foreman of the Dauja Mine. Stubborn, protective, hard-working, addicted
  - Sula, Merchant. Connected, professional, skilled

- **Notable Assets:** Stockpiles of mithril. High quality equipment.
- **Quirks:** Mining mithril requires using dangerous chemicals. The dwarves here purposefully inhale the fumes to connect to their presumed ancestors.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Deepforge Clan]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Echo Wave Riders]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Circle of Stars]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hailstone Clan]]
- **Situation:** The [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hailstone Clan]] put restrictions on mithril exports from [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]] to other [[8. Glossary/Khorvate\|khorvates]] and this has left the Brightstone Clan with product they can't easily sell. This has led to mines shutting down entirely and leaving folks without a livelihood. Magnar Kanuf has struck a deal with the [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Echo Wave Riders]], who will buy their mithril and smuggle it black markets.
- **Goals:**
  - Become the [[8. Glossary/Khorvar]] of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]]
