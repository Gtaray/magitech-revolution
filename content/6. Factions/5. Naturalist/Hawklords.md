A group of dwarves dedicated to protecting the skies above [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. Their duty was more necessary before [[Airship|airships]] could defend themselves against wyverns and such, but now is largely outmoded.

- **Tier:** 2

- **Turf:** The Aerie (HQ). Mountain-top roosts, and all the skies above all of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]].

- **NPCs:**
  - Resh, leader. Handsome, kind, suave
  - Fir, flight captain. Noble, competent, inspiring, tactical
  - Scoops, roost attendant. Young, wide-eyed, eager.

- **Notable Assets:** Roosts of wild giant hawks and eagles

- **Quirks:** Many of the giant eagles and hawks have been awakened by the druids and are sapient.

- **Allies:** [[Captain's Guild]], [[Beasthearts]]

- **Enemies:** [[Stormkiln Clan]], [[The Maelstrom]], [[Earthquake]]

- **Situation:** The skies the Hawklord's call home is getting more and more crowded by the year. They've resorted to extorting money from ships flying near their turf. Word is they're looking to steal an [[Airship|airship]] to solidify their control over the mountain tops. They have even formed a working relationship with the [[Captain's Guild]] and will occasionally be contracted to work for them.

- **Goals:**
  - Steal an airship
