A small enclave of secretive druids who read portents in the stars, dedicated to seeking a set of ancient mystical artifacts

- **Tier:** 2
- **Turf:** [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]] and [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]]. A small hideout in the hills near [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Giant's Fall]] (HQ)
- **NPCs:**
  - Doraam, Keeper of Sight. Masked, mysterious, driven.
  - Ismissa. Robed, unarmed, magically powerful
  - Rakeen. Fighter, physical, huge, brutal

- **Notable Assets:** Druidic powers, mostly stellar. Physical prowess via wild shape. A collection of magical artifacts.
- **Quirks:** Members' eyes glow with starlight at night and wield potent magic. Most where masks. Some say they speak to voices that come from the stars.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthen Enclave]]
- **Situation:** Though few, each member is a powerful druid. They hire themselves out to powerful individuals while searching for a set of relics called the Raiment of Night. Doraam has become Magnar Kosoth [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan\|Bloodhammer]]'s personal mystic and advisor after sensing a piece of the Raiment - the Aleph Key.
- **Goals:**
  - Quietly acquire The Aleph Key
