The ruling clan of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]] and one of the oldest clans in all of [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], predating the republic by over a millennia if they're to be believed. Some of the staunchest holders of ancient ways and fiercest opponents of the magitech revolution.

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Taj]] (City, HQ). All towns and outposts in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]].
- **NPCs:**
	- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Juska Hailstone. Serious, traditional, responsible
	- Tarja Hailstone, Accountant. Addicted, connected, demanding.
	- Zefon Hailstone, Guide. Druid, quiet, selfless.

- **Notable Assets:** All of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]]'s trade (furs, minerals, lumber, meat, etc.), untapped natural resources.
- **Quirks:** The Hailstone clan has banned all magitech in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]]. While they do not seek to punish for every offense, make enough noise and they will seek recompense.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthen Enclave]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Beasthearts]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Ivory Throne]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Sender's Guild]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Bloodhammer Clan]]
- **Situation:** The Hailstone clan is in a precarious position; they know the location of several untapped [[8. Glossary/Mithril]] veins but refuse to mine them in protest. [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers\|Guilders]] have been caught illegally prospecting in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]] and that has heated tensions quite a bit. Seems only a matter of time before it comes to blows, and they have to be preparing.
- **Goals:**
	- Stockpile magical equipment and relics for any potential conflicts
