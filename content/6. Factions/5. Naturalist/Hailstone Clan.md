The ruling clan of [[Oghrann]] and one of the oldest clans in all of [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]], predating the republic by over a millennia if they're to be believed. Some of the staunchest holders of ancient ways and fiercest opponents of the magitech revolution.

- **Tier:** 3

- **Turf:** [[Taj]] (City, HQ). All towns and outposts in [[Oghrann]].

- **NPCs:**
  - [[Magnar]] Juska Hailstone. Serious, traditional, responsible
  - Tarja Hailstone, Accountant. Addicted, connected, demanding.
  - Zefon Hailstone, Guide. Druid, quiet, selfless.

- **Notable Assets:** All of [[Oghrann]]'s trade (furs, minerals, lumber, meat, etc.), untapped natural resources.

- **Quirks:** The Hailstone clan has banned all magitech in [[Oghrann]]. While they do not seek to punish for every offense, make enough noise and they will seek recompense.

- **Allies:** [[Earthen Enclave]], [[Beasthearts]], [[The Ivory Throne]]

- **Enemies:** [[Sender's Guild]], [[21st Squadron]], [[Bloodhammer Clan]]

- **Situation:** The Hailstone clan is in a precarious position; they know the location of several untapped [[Mithril]] veins but refuse to mine them in protest. [[Guild of Engineers|Guilders]] have been caught illegally prospecting in [[Oghrann]] and that has heated tensions quite a bit. Seems only a matter of time before it comes to blows, and they have to be preparing.

- **Goals:**
  - Stockpile magical equipment and relics for any potential conflicts
