A terrorist organization bent on stopping the widespread spiritual enslavement that magitech demands.

- **Tier:** 2

- **Turf:** An abandoned warehouse (HQ). Secret bunkers in big cities.

- **NPCs:**
  - "Lore", Leader. Driven, spirit-speaker, druid.
  - "Shanks", Mercenary. Skilled duelist, soldier, quiet.
  - "Tax", Mechanic. Grumpy, obsessed.

- **Notable Assets:** A stock of old [[Exosuit|exosuits]] and [[Firearms|firearms]].

- **Quirks:** This group is too extreme for the [[Earthen Enclave]], causing the enclave disavow Earthquake's actions.

- **Allies:** [[Deepforge Clan]], [[Draxler's Raiders]], [[The Maelstrom]], [[Beasthearts]]

- **Enemies:** [[21st Squadron]], [[Amberspire Clan]], [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Earthen Enclave]]

- **Situation:** Earthquake is a small operation that's looking to bolster it's ranks, but getting in isn't easy. They're fighting in the name of protecting the land's spiritual energies and take offense at most anything the industrialists do. They aren't above using magitech themselves, however. They've got quite the stockpile of [[Exosuit|exosuits]], though it's unclear what for.

- **Goals:**
  - Steal the Hammer of Stakros from the [[Earthen Enclave]]
  - Destroy industrialist infrastructure (on repeat)
