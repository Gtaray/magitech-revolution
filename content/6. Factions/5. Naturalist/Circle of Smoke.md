A group of druids and shamans who serve the realm by managing the land to avoid catastrophic wildfires. They are equal parts prevention and response, making sure that wildfires are less likely to start, and also by containing them once they have. They may also be a cult.

- **Tier:** 1
- **Turf:** A small settlement on the shores of Lake Mead near [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Overlook]] in [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Etar Nökor (King's Crest)]] (HQ). Watchtowers dotting the hills and mountains of woodland areas.
- **NPCs:**
	- Kurgan, Keeper of Smoke. Leader, easy-going, friendly
	- Kulet, Attendant. Scheming, powerful, believer
	- Zutthan, Watcher. Wanderer, insightful, druid
	- Grumdi, Captain. Helpful, wild, mechanic.

- **Notable Assets:** Goodwill and friends everywhere. "Raincloud", a small [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]]. Strong magic.
- **Quirks:** The Circle of Smoke are friends to all and welcomed in any city. They don't play politics, and are always willing to help.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthen Enclave]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Stormkiln Clan]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hawklords]]
- **Enemies:** None
- **Situation:** Thus far the Circle of Smoke hasn't declared any particular way in the growing tension between industry and traditions. There are whispers that the Circle's true intention is to commune with [[8. Glossary/Natural Spirits#Downpour Drenches the World\|Downpour Drenches the World]] and bring about a catastrophic flood, in order to wash away the stains of industry
- **Goals:**
	- Commune with [[8. Glossary/Natural Spirits#Downpour Drenches the World\|Downpour Drenches the World]]
