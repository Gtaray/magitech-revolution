All factions have a tier rating that represents their overall power, influence, and wealth. The higher tier the faction, the scarier they are to go up against, as that tier number is compared against for most things the crew will do.

If a faction is one tier above your [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]], then by default all of your actions against them are one level less effective (normal to limited [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/4. Effect\|effect]]). If a faction is 2 or more tiers above your [[3. The Crew/5. Crew Quality\|Crew Quality]], then by default all of your actions against them are 2 levels less effective (normal to no [[1. How to Play/3. Threat Roll/4. Effect\|effect]]).
