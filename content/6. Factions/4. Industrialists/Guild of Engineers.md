The singular largest guild in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]], responsible for resource acquisition, [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airship]] and [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuit]] manufacture, magitech R&D, and more.

- **Tier:** 5
- **Turf:** [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Dur Olo]] in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]] (HQ). There is a Guildhall in every moderate sized town or city. Shipyard in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Giant's Fall]], [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]].
- **NPCs:**
  - Storn Ironroot, Guildmaster. Megalomaniac, deals with Ajax
  - Torra Saltstone, Chief of Operations. Prosthetic eye (sees all), connected, influential, unscrupulous
  - Yori Thunderbrand, Chief of Research. Genius, reclusive, overworked.

- **Notable Assets:** Magitech even the government is unaware of. Secret research laboratories.
- **Quirks:** Many high-ranking guild members sport custom prosthetics.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Deeprock]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Cobalt Syndicate]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Spark of Life]]
- **Situation:** With members across [[8. Glossary/Khett]], the Guild of Engineers are beyond the ability for individual Magnar's to police. The Guild uses this to worm their way into all facets of life. Anyone who does any work vaguely related to magitech has to pay their dues to the Guild. They're known to hire thugs to enforce their control of the market.
- **Goal:**
  - Build [[8. Glossary/Mithril\|mithril]] mines in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Oghrann]]
