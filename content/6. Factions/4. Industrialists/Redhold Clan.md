Operates the one and only currency mint as well as the largest bank in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]].

- **Tier:** 4
- **Turf:** Redhold Bank in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Ramthas]], [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]] (HQ). Currency exchanges in all major cities. Secret caches of mysterious items in magically protected vaults. 
- **NPCs:**
	- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Uroq Redhold. Calculating, ruthless, expansionary.
	- Korsa Redhold. Operations head, conniving, jealous, sly.
	- Broq Redhold. Franchise owner, honorable, precise.

- **Notable Assets:** Numerous secret vaults that store wonderous items. More money than any other guild.
- **Quirks:** All money that goes through the bank is meticulously tracked, making it very difficult to use the bank for criminal purposes. The clan harshly punishes its own members who break the law.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Green Dragons]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Dragonshield Clan]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Vorex]]
- **Situation:** [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Uroq is playing Guild politics. He hopes to show that the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] has to much to handle, and that the Redhold Clan should handle sales of their raw materials and supplies. He's spent vast sums on this, betting he'll get it back once the clan takes over.
- **Goals:** 
	- Disrupt the Guild of Engineers' Mining Operation
