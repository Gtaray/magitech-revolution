A young clan that have mastered the art of capturing lightning from storm clouds and using it to give life to [[Spark|Sparks]]; thinking automatons much more sophisticated than normal constructs.

- **Tier:** 2

- **Turf:** [[Steel City]] (HQ). Spark factory and assorted workshops in larger towns. A single platinum mine.

- **NPCs:**
  - [[Magnar]] Kharo Stormkiln. Ambitious, genius, demanding.
  - Khazik, captain. Lightning wrangler, cocky, daredevil
  - Brux, alchemist. Overworked, underpaid, snarky.

- **Notable Assets:** The only [[Spark]] workshop. Dozens (hundreds?) of [[Spark]] guard and workers. Airships kitted to ride the lightning, and the skilled crews to fly them.

- **Quirks:** The lightning they harness is said to come from a powerful [[Natural Spirits#Lightning Splits the Sky|spirit of storm & sky]]. Have they angered it?

- **Allies:** [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Amberspire Clan]]

- **Enemies:** [[Spark of Life]], [[Draxler's Raiders]], [[Earthen Enclave]]

- **Situation:** While young, the Stormkiln Clan has made a name for themselves with the invention of the [[Spark|Sparks]]. They've been catapulted into the [[Guild of Engineers]]' sights and have to live up to the high expectations placed on them. This enormous pressure is driving them to expand their work, perhaps a bit recklessly. In their search for the lost ruins of Skaz Kor one of their airships has gone missing. And there are rumors that they've captured druids and shamans who they force to work for them.

- **Goals:**
  - Find the lost ruin of Skaz Kor, which holds ancient engineering knowledge.
