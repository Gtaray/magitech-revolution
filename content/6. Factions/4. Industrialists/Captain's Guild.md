A security guild started by a former [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]] captain. This guild hires out independent skyship crews to clients who have need. Backed by the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]] (the Bank) because they offer insurance on the goods they're protecting.

This guild fills the niche left by the federalization of the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]]. Since the squadron was responsible for keeping the skies safe, airship crime has increased, and crew's were bidding against each other for protection jobs, driving prices down. Thus was formed the Captain's Guild.

Guild halls in major cities offer a place for crews to stay for free, and they negotiate skydock rates for crews as well.

- **Tier:** 2
- **Turf:** HQ in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Orinth]]. Guildhalls in major cities in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Ladur]], [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]], and [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Torhmek]]

- **Quirks:** The Captain's Guild is first and foremost, a network. Its core membership is very small, but its ties to other factions and crews is extensive. Some say they even have connections to criminal gangs.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Redhold Clan]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Hawklords]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Ivory Throne]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/The Concordiat]]
- **Enemies:**  [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Heartfire Clan]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]]
- **Situation:** Those wishing to hire the services of an airship crew can contact the Captain's Guild, who will negotiate price. Then the Guild connects the client with an airship crew that meets their needs for the job. The Captain's Guild then takes a portion of the profits. 
- **Goals:**
	- Expand their roster of contracted crews.
	- Poach experienced [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/21st Squadron]] captains and crews
