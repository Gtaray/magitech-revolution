Fourth and fifth children, these young nobles have taken an oath sanctified by the republic to seek the Worldsoul.

- **Tier:** 1
- **Turf:** None. These wanderer-warriors have no home, save a bar called The Grail in [[7. Duradin/3. Territories/Dåtan Tor (Iron Shores)]] where they leave messages for each other.
- **NPCs:**
	- Algrim Steelson. Leader, glib, hopeful, believer.
	- Gwenvia Sunblood. Priestess, renowned, bejeweled
	- Brand Bucklebrew. Explorer, death wish, restless.
	- Lokum Copperbottle. Religious, vigilant, honorable

- **Notable Assets:** An ancient map of the Khettish Steppes from when the giants ruled the land.
- **Quirks:** Each Concordant is as distinct as they can be from each other. This tight-knit group is comprised of unique, colorful characters.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Beasthearts]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Circle of Smoke]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Acolytes of Many]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthquake]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Iron Sights]]
- **Situation:** A discovered message about a powerful relic from the Age of Fire that could reshape entire worlds ignited a fervor in a number of noble youths. Algrim recently found a marked map of the steppes predating the republic and the Concordiat seek to unravel its mysteries.
- **Goals:**
	- Find first lost site on their map
