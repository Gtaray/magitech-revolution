The ruling clan of [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]]. A clan filled with some of the best alchemist and magitechnicians in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Nearly every technological invention has an Amberspire seal on it somewhere

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Dur Olo]] (HQ), as well as all major cities in [[7. Duradin/2. Khorvates/Durvos]]
- **NPCs:**
	- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Ingor Amberspire. Visionary, ambitious, inventive
	- Sargo, lab boss. Micromanager, results-focused, greedy
	- Rokkum, alchemist. Intelligent, hard-worker, has a family

- **Notable Assets:** The best alchemists in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. High quality magitech components. Money, influence, and connections.
- **Quirks:** Amberspire have all sorts of unreleased and experimental stuff they're working on.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Stormkiln Clan]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/The Maelstrom]], [[6. Factions/5. Naturalist/Earthquake]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]]
- **Situation:** The Amberspire's are the foremost experts on the alchemical side of magitech, and work closely with the [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] when developing new products. They are close to cracking the secret magics of the Storm Giant cloud cities, and are working on new formula for military [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airships]] and [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuits]]. Some way they have a secret lab where they perform unethical and illegal experiments.
- **Goals:**
	- Find a storm giant city
