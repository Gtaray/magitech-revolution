The ruling clan of [[Durvos]]. A clan filled with some of the best alchemist and magitechnicians in [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. Nearly every technological invention has an Amberspire seal on it somewhere

- **Tier:** 3

- **Turf:** [[Dur Olo]] (HQ), as well as all major cities in [[Durvos]]

- **NPCs:**
  - [[Magnar]] Ingor Amberspire. Visionary, ambitious, inventive
  - Sargo, lab boss. Micromanager, results-focused, greedy
  - Rokkum, alchemist. Intelligent, hard-worker, has a family

- **Notable Assets:** The best alchemists in [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. High quality magitech components. Money, influence, and connections.

- **Quirks:** Amberspire have all sorts of unreleased and experimental stuff they're working on.

- **Allies:** [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Stormkiln Clan]]

- **Enemies:** [[The Maelstrom]], [[Earthquake]], [[Ashen Knives]]

- **Situation:** The Amberspire's are the foremost experts on the alchemical side of magitech, and work closely with the [[Guild of Engineers]] when developing new products. They are close to cracking the secret magics of the Storm Giant cloud cities, and are working on new formula for military [[Airship|airships]] and [[Exosuit|exosuits]]. Some way they have a secret lab where they perform unethical and illegal experiments.

- **Goals:**
  - Find a storm giant city
