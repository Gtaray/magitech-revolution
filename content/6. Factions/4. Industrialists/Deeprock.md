A paramilitary mercenary company that runs the [[Deeprock Hold]], the highest security prison in [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. Often deals with and brokers communication for those with power and wealth.

- **Tier:** 3

- **Turf:** [[Deeprock Hold]] max security prison in [[Siege Rock]]

- **NPCs:**
  - Mogdin Anvilhelm, Warden. Haughty, confident, wealthy
  - Lund Amberspire, Guard Captain. Muscled, angry
  - Dr. Garon Grimnail, Head Doctor, psychotherapist, corrupt, gambler.

- **Notable Assets:** The biggest prison in [[1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin|Duradin]]. An organized file of dirt on anyone they do business with. Labor for all those imprisoned

- **Quirks:** The staff at [[Deeprock Hold]] are all corrupt and will take bribes for almost anything.

- **Allies:** [[Guild of Engineers]], [[Ashen Knives]]

- **Enemies:** [[Draxler's Raiders]], [[Broadsheet Guild]], [[Draxler's Raiders]]

- **Situation:** Between the Warden's excessive spending habits, Dr. Garon's gambling problem, and Lund's 'educating' of the prisoners, Deeprock always needs more funds and bodies. Deeprock spends a lot of time lobbying local governors to increase sentencing or acquire "special guests" from factions, and just as much time looking for bribes.

- **Goal:**
  - Get more prisoners (on repeat)
