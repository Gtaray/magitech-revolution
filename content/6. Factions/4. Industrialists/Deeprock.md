A paramilitary mercenary company that runs the [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Deeprock Hold]], the highest security prison in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Often deals with and brokers communication for those with power and wealth.

- **Tier:** 3
- **Turf:** [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Deeprock Hold]] max security prison in [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Siege Rock]]
- **NPCs:**
	- Mogdin Anvilhelm, Warden. Haughty, confident, wealthy
	- Lund Amberspire, Guard Captain. Muscled, angry
	- Dr. Garon Grimnail, Head Doctor, psychotherapist, corrupt, gambler.

- **Notable Assets:** The biggest prison in [[7. Duradin/1. Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. An organized file of dirt on anyone they do business with. Labor for all those imprisoned
- **Quirks:** The staff at [[7. Duradin/4. Cities/Deeprock Hold]] are all corrupt and will take bribes for almost anything.
- **Allies:** [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Ashen Knives]]
- **Enemies:** [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]], [[6. Factions/4. Industrialists/Broadsheet Guild]], [[6. Factions/6. Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]]
- **Situation:** Between the Warden's excessive spending habits, Dr. Garon's gambling problem, and Lund's 'educating' of the prisoners, Deeprock always needs more funds and bodies. Deeprock spends a lot of time lobbying local governors to increase sentencing or acquire "special guests" from factions, and just as much time looking for bribes.
- **Goal:**
	- Get more prisoners (on repeat)
