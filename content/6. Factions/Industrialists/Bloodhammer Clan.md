The ruling clan of [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Torhmek]]. Their aggressive warrior-culture keeps the clan squabbles to a minimum, and protects their lands from the monsters that roam it.

**Tier:** 3
**Turf:** Cities and outposts in [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Torhmek]]. Has their clan house in [[7. Duradin/Cities/Giant's Fall]].
**NPCs:**
- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Kosoth Bloodhammer. Strong, protective, terrifying.
- Tyro Bloodhammer. "Troubleshooter", sporting, experienced
- Brond Bloodhammer. Outpost manager, unflappable, connected.

**Notable Assets:** Countless dwarves ready and eager to cause violence. Trained monster hunters (they can hunt people to). Influence in all of [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Torhmek]]
**Quirks:** The Bloodhammer Clan is the [[8. Glossary/Khorvar]] of [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Torhmek]], but they don't much act like it. They don't enact and enforce laws, and they don't provide much for the well-being of their people. Instead, the Bloodhammers collects their tithe and promises two things: the freedom to live however you want, and protection from the wild monsters that get too close.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Circle of Stars]], [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Deeprock]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Criminal/The Ivory Throne]]
**Situation:** The [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] has a growing interest in ancient relics and artifacts, and has been searching and collecting them for the last few years. He hopes this will give him the edge in the cloak and dagger world of inner politics.

- Acquire the Aleph Key
