A paramilitary mercenary company that runs the [[7. Duradin/Cities/Deeprock Hold]], the highest security prison in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Often deals with and brokers communication for those with power and wealth.

**Tier:** 3
**Turf:** [[7. Duradin/Cities/Deeprock Hold]] max security prison in [[7. Duradin/Cities/Siege Rock]]
**NPCs:**
- Mogdin Anvilhelm, Warden. Haughty, confident, wealthy
- Lund Amberspire, Guard Captain. Muscled, angry
- Dr. Garon Grimnail, Head Doctor, psychotherapist, corrupt, gambler.

**Notable Assets:** The biggest prison in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. An organized file of dirt on anyone they do business with. Labor for all those imprisoned
**Quirks:** The staff at [[7. Duradin/Cities/Deeprock Hold]] are all corrupt and will take bribes for almost anything.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Ashen Knives]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]], [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Broadsheet Guild]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Draxler's Raiders]]
**Situation:** Between the Warden's excessive spending habits, Dr. Garon's gambling problem, and Lund's 'educating' of the prisoners, Deeprock always needs more funds and bodies. Deeprock spends a lot of time lobbying local governors to increase sentencing or acquire "special guests" from factions, and just as much time looking for bribes.
**Goal:**
- Get more prisoners (on repeat)
