The ruling clan of [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Durvos]]. A clan filled with some of the best alchemist and magitechnicians in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. Nearly every technological invention has an Amberspire seal on it somewhere

**Tier:** 3
**Turf:** [[7. Duradin/Cities/Dur Olo]] (HQ), as well as all major cities in [[7. Duradin/Khorvates/Durvos]]
**NPCs:**
- [[8. Glossary/Magnar]] Ingor Amberspire. Visionary, ambitious, inventive
- Sargo, lab boss. Micromanager, results-focused, greedy
- Rokkum, alchemist. Intelligent, hard-worker, has a family

**Notable Assets:** The best alchemists in [[7. Duradin/Dwarven Republic of Duradin\|Duradin]]. High quality magitech components. Money, influence, and connections.
**Quirks:** Amberspire have all sorts of unreleased and experimental stuff they're working on.
**Allies:** [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]], [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Stormkiln Clan]]
**Enemies:** [[6. Factions/Criminal/The Maelstrom]], [[6. Factions/Naturalist/Earthquake]], [[6. Factions/Criminal/Ashen Knives]]
**Situation:** The Amberspire's are the foremost experts on the alchemical side of magitech, and work closely with the [[6. Factions/Industrialists/Guild of Engineers]] when developing new products. They are close to cracking the secret magics of the Storm Giant cloud cities, and are working on new formula for military [[8. Glossary/Airship\|airships]] and [[8. Glossary/Exosuit\|exosuits]]. Some way they have a secret lab where they perform unethical and illegal experiments.
